## §15 Semidirect products and SES (lines 2417–2519)
- **Verified.**

- **Wrong/dubious. SEVERITY: HIGH.**
  - **Trivial-twist identification is wrong when n is even.** Line 2504:
    ```
    `ℤ/${n} × ℤ/2 = ℤ/${2*n/gcd(n,2)} (abelian, direct product)`
    ```
    The expression `2*n/gcd(n,2)` equals `lcm(n,2)`, **not** the order of the group. For n even, `lcm(n,2) = n`, so the widget displays e.g.:
    - n=2: "Z/2 × Z/2 = Z/2" — wrong (should be V₄, not Z/2).
    - n=4: "Z/4 × Z/2 = Z/4" — wrong (Z/4 × Z/2 has order 8 and is not cyclic).
    - n=6: "Z/6 × Z/2 = Z/6" — wrong.
    - n=8: "Z/8 × Z/2 = Z/8" — wrong.
    - n=10: "Z/10 × Z/2 = Z/10" — wrong.
    For odd n the formula accidentally gives the right cyclic group via CRT (n=3: Z/6 ✓; n=5: Z/10 ✓; n=7: Z/14 ✓; n=9: Z/18 ✓). The correct identification: Z/n × Z/2 is cyclic Z/{2n} iff gcd(n,2)=1; when n is even it is **not** cyclic and remains as Z/n × Z/2 (or, equivalently, Z/lcm(n,2) × Z/gcd(n,2) = Z/n × Z/2).
  - Range of n slider is [2,10], so this bug is hit on 5 of 9 inputs (n = 2, 4, 6, 8, 10).
- **Underspecified.** None.
- **Severity. HIGH** — outputs an incorrect group identification that is also wrong about cardinality (claims order n, actual order 2n) for half of the slider range.
