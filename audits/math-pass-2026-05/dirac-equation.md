## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`dirac-equation.html:957` — Pauli equation σ·B sign inconsistency.** With the convention $\vec\pi=\vec p-e\vec A$ used in line 943, $[\pi^j,\pi^k]=-ie\epsilon^{jkl}B^l$ gives $i\vec\sigma\cdot(\vec\pi\times\vec\pi)/(2m) = +\frac{e}{2m}\vec\sigma\cdot\vec B$. The boxed Pauli equation writes $-\frac{e}{2m}\vec\sigma\cdot\vec B$. The very next sentence (line 959) then says $\vec\mu=(e/2m)\vec\sigma$ with **no** minus, which would only be consistent if the σ·B term in the Hamiltonian were $-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B = -(e/2m)\vec\sigma\cdot\vec B$ — but that requires $e$ to mean the (negative) electron charge, which contradicts $\vec\pi=\vec p-e\vec A$ with $e>0$. Either fix the sign in the boxed equation to `+e/(2m)σ·B` or globally adopt `e<0` for electron and rewrite $\vec\pi=\vec p-e\vec A$ commentary. As written the two adjacent equations disagree by a sign.
- **`dirac-equation.html:1050` — $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)$ (over $\mathbb{R}$).** This is **false as a real Lie algebra isomorphism**. As a real Lie algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})$ is $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$ (six-dimensional, simple, non-compact). $\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)$ is $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ (six-dimensional, semisimple, compact) — different signature, different reality structure. The correct statement is at the **complexified** level: $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)_\mathbb{C}\cong\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})$, equivalently $\mathfrak{su}(2)_\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_\mathbb{C}$. The "(over $\mathbb{R}$)" parenthetical is exactly the wrong qualifier — should be "(after complexification)" or "(as complex Lie algebras)".

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`dirac-equation.html:402` γ⁰ display matrix.** Rendered as `\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}` — in 2×2 block form the entries are 2×2 identity blocks $I_2$ and $-I_2$, but a reader may parse the LaTeX as a literal 2×2 with scalar entries. The widget readout (line 501) clarifies as "γ⁰ = diag(+1,+1,−1,−1)". Pedagogically OK; mathematically the block-form convention is standard but worth flagging.
- **`dirac-equation.html:743` v-spinor spin labelling.** Widget uses $v(p_-,\uparrow)$ with upper = $(0, p_-/(|E_-|+m))$ and lower = $(1,0)$. Many texts label this $v(p,\downarrow)$ (the $v$-spinor's spin index is the positron spin, opposite to the electron spin appearing in the $\eta_s$ slot). The pedagogical claim being verified ($\psi^\dagger\psi\ge 0$) is robust to this convention, but the label "↑" is convention-dependent.
- **Hydrogen fine-structure / Dirac formula** — not present on the page (the audit prompt asked about it; the topic page does not address it). Not a claim to verify.
- **Weyl / Majorana basis realizations** — only mentioned in passing; no explicit matrix realizations given. Not a claim to verify.
