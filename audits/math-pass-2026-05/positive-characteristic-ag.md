# Math-correctness audit — `positive-characteristic-ag.html`

## Verified claims

**§1 Absolute / relative Frobenius.** $F_X$ identity on points, $f \mapsto f^p$ on sections; $X^{(p)} = X \times_{k,\sigma} k$; relative $F_{X/k}: X \to X^{(p)}$ unique $k$-morphism with $F_X$ as composition through structure projection. Correct. $F_*\mathcal{O}_X$ locally free of rank $p^n$ on smooth $n$-fold over perfect $k$ (Kunz) — correct, including the local basis $\{x_1^{i_1}\cdots x_n^{i_n} : 0 \le i_j < p\}$.

**§2 Frobenius splittings / Mehta–Ramanathan.** Definition of split (retraction with $\varphi(1)=1$) — correct. Vanishing argument: $F^*L = L^{\otimes p}$ (absolute Frobenius pulls a line bundle to its $p$-th tensor power), projection formula, summand argument, Serre vanishing on $L^{-p^r}$ — all correct. Raynaud 1978 counterexample to Kodaira in char $p$ — correct attribution. Mehta–Ramanathan 1985 / Brion–Kumar reference — correct.

**§3 Cartier operator.** Cartier 1957 isomorphism $C^{-1}: \Omega^i_{X^{(p)}/k} \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathcal{H}^i(F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k})$ characterised by $d\bar{x} \mapsto [x^{p-1}\,dx]$ — correct. Deligne–Illusie 1987 lifting to $\bigoplus_i \Omega^i_{X/k}[-i] \xrightarrow{\sim} F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}$ under $W_2(k)$-lift — correct. The `polyToString` widget mechanics (extract terms with $k \equiv p-1 \pmod p$) match the Cartier operator on $\Omega^1$ correctly.

**§5 Artin–Schreier.** $y^p - y = f(x)$, Galois $\mathbb{Z}/p$ generated by $y \mapsto y+1$ — correct. Genus formula $g = (p-1)(m-1)/2$ for $\gcd(m,p)=1$, $f$ with single pole of order $m$ at $\infty$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$ — correct (Riemann–Hurwitz with one wild branch). Reduction $y \mapsto y - x^j$ when $m = pj$ — correct. Inertia $p$-part / Swan conductor / Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich attribution — correct.

**§6 Witt vectors / de Rham–Witt.** $W(\mathbb{F}_p) = \mathbb{Z}_p$, $W(\mathbb{F}_{p^n})$ unramified deg-$n$ extension — correct. Carry polynomial $C_p(a_0,b_0) = ((a_0+b_0)^p - a_0^p - b_0^p)/p \in \mathbb{Z}[a_0,b_0]$ — correct. $F: W_{n+1}\Omega^j \to W_n\Omega^j$, $V: W_n\Omega^j \to W_{n+1}\Omega^j$, $FV = VF = p$ — correct. Bloch–Illusie–Deligne $H^*_{\mathrm{cris}}(X/W) = \mathbb{H}^*(X_{\mathrm{Zar}}, W\Omega^\bullet_X)$ — correct.

**§7 Pathologies.** Raynaud counterexample to Kodaira; Hironaka 1964; resolution open in char $p$ for $\dim \ge 4$; surfaces (Abhyankar–Lipman) and threefolds (Cossart–Piltant) — all correct. Hirokado 1999 / Schröer 2003 non-liftable examples in char 2,3 — correct.

## Wrong / dubious claims

**`:555` F-regular row of table — definition garbled.** "every $x \in \bar I$ and $c \notin$ minimal primes, $cx^{p^e} \in I^{[p^e]}$ fails to hold." The defining condition for $x \in I^*$ is: there exists $c$ (not in any minimal prime) with $cx^{p^e} \in I^{[p^e]}$ for all $e \gg 0$. F-regular means $I^* = I$ for every ideal $I$ in every localization — the "fails to hold" phrasing inverts the quantifier and yields nonsense. Should read: "for every $I$, $I^* = I$" or "the only elements satisfying the tight-closure condition are those already in $I$."

**`:556` F-rational row — definition wrong.** "only the unit ideal is tightly closed at the parameter ideal of every system." F-rational means every parameter ideal (in a Cohen–Macaulay local ring) is tightly closed, i.e., $\mathfrak{q}^* = \mathfrak{q}$ for parameter ideals. The current phrasing is inverted/garbled.

**`:583` Cusp $y^2 = x^3$ "F-pure iff $p \equiv 1 \pmod 6$".** Fedder's criterion says $k[x,y]/(f)$ is F-pure iff $f^{p-1} \notin (x^p, y^p)$. For $f = y^2 - x^3$, the term $\binom{p-1}{k}(-1)^k y^{2(p-1-k)} x^{3k}$ avoids $(x^p, y^p)$ only if $3k < p$ and $2(p-1-k) < p$, forcing $(p-1)/2 \le k < p/3$ — empty for all $p \ge 3$, and $p=2$ also fails directly. The cusp is **never** F-pure. The "$p \equiv 1 \pmod 6$" criterion likely got conflated with the ordinary/supersingular dichotomy for the elliptic curve $y^2 = x^3 + 1$ (ordinary iff $p \equiv 1 \pmod 3$).

**`:584` Cone $xy - z^p$ "not F-split in characteristic $p$ exactly".** Fedder: $f^{p-1} = (xy - z^p)^{p-1}$ contains the $k=0$ term $(xy)^{p-1} = x^{p-1}y^{p-1}$ (coefficient 1), which is not in $(x^p, y^p, z^p)$. So $xy - z^p$ **is** F-pure in char $p$. The widget's verdict is wrong.

**`:833` Bertini failure description.** "A generic hyperplane section of $\mathbb{P}^n$ via the Frobenius is everywhere singular" conflates two failures. The classical Bertini theorem on smoothness of generic hyperplane sections **holds** in char $p$ for smooth varieties. What fails in char $p$ is Bertini for arbitrary base-point-free linear systems (e.g., the ones induced by inseparable maps to projective space). The example given — "hyperplane section via Frobenius" — is really about pulling back along an inseparable morphism, not a hyperplane section in the original sense.

**`:835` Mumford char-2 surface "$h^{0,1}=0$ but $h^{1,0}=1$".** Mumford's pathological surfaces in char $p$ exhibit $h^{0,1} \ne h^{1,0}$, but the specific numerical assignment given is unverified — the standard direction of the asymmetry is $h^{1,0} > h^{0,1}$ (e.g., supersingular K3-like or Enriques-like examples) but checking the exact $(0,1)$ Mumford construction needs literature lookup; treat the specific numbers as suspect.

**`:835` "$W_2$-lifts always exist for smooth proper $X_0$ of dimension $\le 3$ (Liedtke–Satriano)."** Not quite. Liedtke–Satriano work on Witt-vector lifting addresses obstructions but does not give an unconditional "$W_2$ exists in dim $\le 3$" statement; in fact non-$W_2$-liftable smooth threefolds in char 2 are known. The blanket attribution overstates the result.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`:553` "F-pure ↔ log-canonical" / `:555` "F-regular ↔ klt" / `:556` "F-rational ↔ rational" char-0 analogs.** True under reduction mod $p \gg 0$ (Hara–Watanabe, Hara–Yoshida, Smith), as the §4 reduction-mod-$p$ note correctly recovers, but the column header just says "char-0 analog" without the asymptotic-$p$ caveat — an isolated singularity can be klt in char 0 yet have F-singular type at finitely many bad primes.
- **`:597` "Hara–Mehta–Smith"** for the klt ↔ F-regular bridge — usually credited Hara–Watanabe (klt ↔ F-regular) and Smith / Hara (rational ↔ F-rational), not Hara–Mehta–Smith as a triple.
- **`:678` Widget label "conductor $m+1$".** This is the Artin conductor (= Swan + 1 tame part). The Swan conductor proper is $m$. Both conventions exist; the widget should pick one and label it.
- **`:781` "FV = VF = multiplication by $p$"** stated for the de Rham–Witt complex — true on $W_n\Omega^j$ for $j \ge 1$, but the relation on $W_n\mathcal{O}$ ($j=0$) is the same. Description is correct but the slope-raising parenthetical "(raising slopes by $p$)" at line 727 should read "$F$ multiplies the Frobenius slope by $p$" or similar — as written it's ambiguous.
- **No discussion of ordinary vs supersingular elliptic curves.** The audit prompt asks for verification of this dichotomy but the page does not treat it explicitly (it appears tangentially via the Hartshorne–Speiser–Lyubeznik / Hasse-invariant context but no concrete elliptic-curve example is given).
- **No discussion of inseparable extensions** as a topic, beyond the Bertini parenthetical. The page focuses on Frobenius geometry rather than the Galois-theoretic side.
- **§5 quiz / cuspidal-curve example interplay.** The genus formula widget is correct for AS covers branched at $\infty$ only; if $f$ has additional finite poles the formula must sum Swan contributions. The widget restricts to monomial $f = x^m$ which has only the $\infty$ pole, so the displayed formula is sound within its scope but the prose generalisation "Riemann–Hurwitz gives $g$ as a function of the Swan conductors at each pole" is the right general statement.

## Severity

**Moderate.** Three quantitative widget errors (cusp F-purity at line 583, cone $xy - z^p$ F-purity at line 584, F-regular/F-rational definitions at lines 555–556) are the kind of mistake a reader would carry away as fact. The F-singularity definitions are the central content of §4 and are currently mis-stated; the cusp example is the most prominent F-singularity example in the widget and gives the wrong characteristic-dependence. Recommend (a) rewriting the F-regular and F-rational table rows to "$I^* = I$ for every (parameter) ideal $I$"; (b) replacing the cusp F-purity claim with a correct statement (cusp is never F-pure; use $y^2 = x^3 + x$ or another example for the $p$-dependent dichotomy); (c) re-checking the cone $xy - z^p$ verdict; (d) softening the Bertini and Liedtke–Satriano claims; (e) adding a brief ordinary-vs-supersingular elliptic-curve remark to address the audit-prompt scope. All of §1 (Frobenius factorization), §2 (M–R proof scrub), §3 (Cartier), §5 (AS genus), §6 (Witt + dRW) are mathematically clean.
