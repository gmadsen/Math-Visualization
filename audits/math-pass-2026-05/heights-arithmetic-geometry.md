# Math-correctness audit — `heights-arithmetic-geometry.html`

Pass: 2026-05.

## Verified claims (sections)

- **§1 Naive height.** `H([x_0:...:x_n]) = max|x_i|` on coprime representative; `H(355/113)=355`. Correct.
- **§2 Logarithmic height + Northcott.** `h = log H`; place-sum normalisation `(1/[K:Q]) sum log max|x_i|_v` is field-independent. Northcott (1949): `{P : h(P)<=B, [Q(P):Q]<=d}` finite. Sharpness commentary (cyclotomics show degree bound is needed) correct. Schanuel asymptotic `12/pi^2 N^2` for `P^1(Q)` confirmed numerically (count of pairs `(a,b)`, `gcd=1`, `b>=1`, `max<=N` matches `12N^2/pi^2 + O(N log N)`).
- **§3 Weil height machine.** `Pic(X) -> {functions}/O(1)` homomorphism; functoriality `h_{f^*L} = h_L o f + O(1)`. Standard. The widget claim `h(f^n(P)) = d^n h(P) + O(1)` for `f(z)=z^d` on `P^1` is correct.
- **§4 Néron–Tate.** `h_L([n]P) = n^2 h_L(P) + O(1)`; Tate averaging `lim h_L([n]P)/n^2`; properties (`hat h([n]P) = n^2 hat h(P)` exactly, parallelogram law, `hat h >= 0` with equality iff torsion, `hat h = h_L + O(1)`). All standard. Regulator as Gram determinant of pairing on `A(K)/tors`. Correct.
- **§5 Mahler measure.** Definition `M(f) = |a| prod max(1,|alpha_i|)`; Jensen's formula expression; `h(alpha) = log M(f) / deg f`. Lehmer polynomial `x^10+x^9-x^7-...+x+1`, `M(L) ≈ 1.17628…` confirmed numerically (root `1.176280818…`). Pisot example `x^2-x-1` gives `M = (1+sqrt 5)/2`. Cyclotomic `x^10-1` gives `M=1`. Kronecker (1857) and Smyth gap statements correct. Dobrowolski bound `1 + c(loglog d/log d)^3` correct.
- **§6 Faltings/Mordell.** Faltings (1983, Mordell 1922): genus `>=2` curves over a number field have finite `C(K)`. Genus dichotomy (`g=0` rational; `g=1` Mordell–Weil; `g>=2` finite) correct. Faltings' isogeny-finiteness w.r.t. `h_F` and the curve `y^2=x^3-x+1` example (LMFDB 92.a1, rank 1, generator `(0,1)`, conductor 92) confirmed.
- **§7 Arakelov.** Metrised line bundles, arithmetic Chow groups, `hat deg : hat CH^d -> R`, height-as-arithmetic-degree formula `hat h_{bar L}(P) = (1/[K:Q]) hat deg(P^* bar L)`. Standard.
- **§9 Vojta + abc.** Vojta inequality form correct; specialisations (Roth on `P^1` with one point; Schmidt on `P^n` with hyperplanes; Faltings on curves with `D=∅`). Specialisation to `(P^1, {0,1,∞})`: `K + D = O(-2) + O(3) = O(1)`, proximity sum becomes `log rad(abc)`, recovers strong abc. Numerically verified all six abc qualities in the widget: `1+8=9` (q≈1.226), `3+125=128` (q≈1.427), `1+4374=4375` with `4374=2·3^7` and `4375=5^4·7` (q≈1.568), Reyssat `2+3^10·109=23^5` (q≈1.6299, current record), `5+27=32` (q≈1.019), `1+2=3` (q≈0.613). All match.
- **§10 Bogomolov / equidistribution.** Manin–Mumford (Raynaud 1983), Bogomolov (Ullmo 1998, Zhang 1998), Yuan–Zhang equidistribution to Haar measure on `A(C)`. Attributions correct. `phi(2310)=480` confirmed. Pila–Zannier strategy and André–Oort attribution standard.

## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:937–940` (§8, descent contraction).** The page derives, from `2P' = P - Q_i` and parallelogram law, that
  ```
  hat h(P') = (1/4) hat h(P - Q_i) <= (1/4)(2 hat h(P) + 2 hat h(Q_i) - hat h(P+Q_i)) <= (1/4) hat h(P) + C
  ```
  with `C = (1/2) max hat h(Q_i)`. The arithmetic in the last step is wrong: dropping `-hat h(P+Q_i)` (which is `<= 0`) gives `(1/4)(2 hat h(P) + 2 hat h(Q_i)) = (1/2) hat h(P) + (1/2) hat h(Q_i)`, NOT `(1/4) hat h(P) + C`. Standard Mordell–Weil descent contracts heights by **factor 1/2 per step**, not 1/4. The downstream geometric-series bound at line 940 inherits the error: it should read `2^{-n} hat h(P) + 2C`, not `4^{-n} hat h(P) + (4/3)C`. (The rest of the section — bounded search radius, terminal Northcott step, conclusion — survives, since the bound `2C` is still independent of `P`.)
- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:548` (§4, illustrative computation).** Claim that on `E : y^2 = x^3 - 2` with `P=(3,5)`, the `x`-coordinate denominators of `[2^k]P` are `4, 121, 18496, …`. Direct computation with the duplication formula gives denominators `100, 58675600, …` — none of `4, 121, 18496` appear. The sequence `4, 121, 18496` (i.e. `2^2, 11^2, 136^2`) does not match this curve+point. Either the curve, the point, or the sequence is wrong; the qualitative claim (digit count grows like `4^k`) is correct, but the concrete numbers are not.
- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:1160` (§9, abc refutation criterion).** "If you ever meet `q>=2`, the abc conjecture is refuted." Strictly, the abc conjecture (`max <= rad^{1+eps}` for every `eps>0`, with **finitely many exceptions** depending on `eps`) is not refuted by a single triple at any fixed quality — only by an infinite family with `q` bounded away from 1, or a triple violating an effective form. A lone `q>=2` is not a refutation. Pedagogical shorthand, but stated as fact.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:519`.** "The bounded-error term … telescopes geometrically." True for the difference `h_L([2^m]P)/4^m - h_L([2^{m-1}]P)/4^{m-1}`, but the page phrases it as a property of the noise rather than the differences. Reader-friendly but slightly informal.
- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:786` (§6, Faltings' isogeny theorem).** Stated as "for each bound C, the set of isogeny classes of abelian varieties over K of dimension g with `h_F <= C` is finite". This is the Northcott property of the Faltings height, conjecturally + provably distinct from the "Faltings' isogeny theorem" (= Tate conjecture for Galois reps of abelian varieties over number fields), which is a different statement in his 1983 paper. The statement here is correct, but the *name* "Faltings' isogeny theorem" is conventionally reserved for the Tate-conjecture half. Minor mislabeling.
- **`heights-arithmetic-geometry.html:1196` (§10, Lehmer ↔ equidistribution).** The argument sketch ("equidistribution forces the Galois orbit to spread on `|z|=1` but discriminants must blow up — contradiction") is allusive; the actual chain (Mignotte / Smyth-style discriminant lower bounds vs. equidistribution speed) is harder than the prose suggests. Not wrong, but underspecified.

## Severity

**Moderate.** One arithmetic error (§8 descent contraction factor of 4 vs. 2) propagated through a derivation, and one fabricated worked-numbers sequence (§4 denominators of `[2^k](3,5)` on `y^2=x^3-2`). Both are isolated to a single sentence/derivation each; the overall pedagogy and theorem statements (Northcott, Néron–Tate, Mahler/Lehmer, Faltings, Vojta/abc, Bogomolov/equidistribution) are correct. Recommended fixes: (1) replace `1/4 → 1/2` and `4^{-n} → 2^{-n}` and `(4/3)C → 2C` in §8; (2) either recompute the actual denominators for `(3,5)` on `y^2=x^3-2` (`100, 58675600, …`) or pick a different curve+point with cleaner numbers, e.g. `y^2 = x^3 - x + 1`, `P=(0,1)` gives denominators `4, 784, 1906277262400, …` whose square roots are integers showing exact `4^k` growth in digit count; (3) soften "the abc conjecture is refuted" to "would be a strong indication against abc" or quantify "an infinite family with q>=2".
