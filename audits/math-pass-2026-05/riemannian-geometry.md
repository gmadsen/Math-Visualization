- **§8 (comparison):** Bonnet–Myers (Ric ≥ (n−1)κ g, complete ⇒ compact, diam ≤ π/√κ, π₁ finite) correct. Cartan–Hadamard (complete, simply-connected, K ≤ 0 ⇒ exp_p a diffeomorphism, no conjugate points, unique geodesic between any two points) correct. Synge's theorem statement correct.

- **§10 (volume):** `dvol_g = √det g · dx¹∧⋯∧dx^n`, `S²` area = 4π, divergence formula `(1/√det g) ∂_i(√det g · X^i)`, Laplace–Beltrami formula, "Δ_g is symmetric and non-positive" (geometers' sign), `α∧⋆β = g(α,β) dvol_g`, codifferential `δ = ±⋆d⋆` all correct.
- **§11 (outro):** bi-invariant metric on a Lie group: `∇_X Y = ½[X,Y]` on left-invariant fields and `K(X,Y) = ¼‖[X,Y]‖²` for orthonormal X,Y both correct.

## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`riemannian-geometry.html:1196`** — Ricci tensor written as `Ric_{ij} = R^k_{kij} = g^{kℓ} R_{kiℓj}`. The first equality is fine, but the lowered-index form is off by a sign relative to the page's own R-conventions. With `R_{ijkℓ} = g_{ℓm} R^m_{ijk}` (line 1178), `R^k_{kij} = g^{kℓ} R_{kijℓ}` (the form actually used at line 1893), not `g^{kℓ} R_{kiℓj}`. The two differ by `R_{kiℓj} = −R_{kijℓ}` (skew in last pair). Internal inconsistency between §5 and §9 statements of the same definition. **Fix:** change line 1196 to `g^{kℓ} R_{kijℓ}` (or equivalently `g^{kℓ} R_{ikjℓ}` after the pair-swap symmetry).
- **`riemannian-geometry.html:1296`** — "by Schur's lemma, pointwise-constant already implies globally constant" with an inline link to the representation-theory Schur's lemma. The statement is correct (it is sometimes called *Schur's theorem* in Riemannian geometry, proved via the second Bianchi identity), but it is **not** Schur's lemma from representation theory; the inline link to `representation-theory.html#schur` mislabels the result. **Fix:** drop the inline link, or rename to "Schur's theorem (Riemannian)" with a different reference.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`riemannian-geometry.html:281`** — Hopf–Rinow stated only as "infimum is attained by a length-minimizing geodesic on a geodesically complete connected manifold." The full theorem's equivalences (geodesic completeness ↔ metric completeness ↔ Heine–Borel ↔ `exp_p` defined on all of `T_p M`, plus the "any two points joined by a minimizing geodesic" conclusion) are missing. Not wrong, but the page promises Hopf–Rinow without delivering its content.
- **Cut locus, conjugate-points-vs-cut-locus distinction, injectivity radius:** entirely absent. The audit prompt asks for these; they have no representation in the page despite being central tools used by Bonnet–Myers (cut radius bound) and Cartan–Hadamard (no conjugate points ⇒ injectivity radius infinite in the universal cover). Underspecified for the topic's stated scope.
- **Rauch comparison theorem** (Jacobi-field bound under sectional-curvature bounds) and **Toponogov's theorem** (geodesic-triangle comparison under sectional-curvature lower bound) are referenced in §8's chapter heading "Comparison theorems" but neither is stated. Only Bonnet–Myers, Synge, and Cartan–Hadamard appear; Bishop–Gromov is named at line 1899 but never stated. The triad most often meant by "comparison theorems" — Rauch / Toponogov / Bishop–Gromov — is not delivered.
- **`riemannian-geometry.html:1813`** — Synge example: "`RP^{2n}` is ruled out as a space of positive sectional curvature." This is true but the obstruction is non-orientability, not failure of Synge's conclusion (Synge takes orientability as a hypothesis). The phrasing reads as if Synge's contrapositive directly excludes `RP^{2n}`. Mild rhetorical slip rather than a math error.
- **`riemannian-geometry.html:1955`** — `K(X,Y) = ¼‖[X,Y]‖²` is given without the explicit "for X,Y orthonormal" qualifier (the formula is stated as if for arbitrary X,Y). Standard reference texts include the orthonormality hypothesis or write the full sectional-curvature ratio.
