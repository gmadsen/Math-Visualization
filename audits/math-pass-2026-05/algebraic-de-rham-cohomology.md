## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **algebraic-de-rham-cohomology.html:838 — total Betti vs Euler characteristic confusion.** Reads: *"Total Betti is `1+2g+1 = 2g+2`, which equals the topological Euler characteristic `χ(C) = 2−2g` plus the alternating-sum reformulation `Σ(−1)^i b_i = 2−2g` ✓."* The total Betti `2g+2` is NOT equal to `χ = 2−2g` (only at `g=0`). The author conflated the sum `Σ b_i` with the alternating sum `Σ(−1)^i b_i`. Only the alternating-sum identity is the Euler characteristic check.
- **algebraic-de-rham-cohomology.html:885 — elliptic-curve "period lattice" overclaim.** *"The two together give the period lattice `Zω₁+Zω₂ ⊂ C` that defines the complex torus."* The period lattice is generated by integrals of the **holomorphic** form `dx/y` over the two homology generators; the second-kind form `x\,dx/y` produces **quasi-periods** (Legendre-relation partners), not lattice generators. Stating that "the two together give the period lattice" is incorrect.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **§1 widget step 6 (line ~321).** "rank-1 module of *generic* rank but rank 1 over the closed point" for `A=k[ε]/ε²`. There is no generic point distinct from the closed point on `Spec A` (a fat point has one point); the "generic vs closed-point rank" framing is muddled. The substantive claim — `Ω¹ ≅ A/(ε) = k` as `A`-module, with `ε`-torsion — is correct.
- **§4 line 579 — "Bott vanishing".** The cited identity `H^q(P^n, Ω^p) = C` iff `p=q≤n` is the Hodge diamond of `P^n` (an immediate corollary of Bott's theorem on `Ω^p(d)`); calling it "Bott vanishing" is loose terminology. Substantively correct.
- **§5 line 670 — K3 derivation.** *"`h^{1,1}=20` comes from `χ(O_X)=2` + Euler `=24` + Noether's formula"* compresses the Hodge-decomposition counting `χ_top = Σ h^{p,q} = 4 + h^{1,1}` into a phrase that mentions Noether's formula without using it. The numbers (`χ(O)=2`, `χ_top=24`, `h^{1,1}=20`) are correct; the derivation as stated is hand-wavy.
- **§6 line 834.** *"`h^{p,q}=h^{q,p}` holds (middle row)"* — the symmetry holds in every cell (corners trivially); restricting it to "middle row" understates it. Cosmetic.
- **Char-p / Frobenius / crystalline coverage.** Page only references these in the §7 outro ("Etale cohomology and motives") and in Deligne–Illusie attribution in §4. No worked Frobenius / crystalline content to audit. Same for Gauss–Manin connection (only implicit in Griffiths-transversality mention) and regulators (one outro reference).
- **Hyperplane arrangements.** Page does not cover Brieskorn / Orlik–Solomon for arrangement complements; only the rank-1 case `G_m = A¹∖{0}` (§3). The user asked about this; it's absent rather than wrong.
