## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **motives.html:594 — "Modulo (B) + (C), hom = num."** The standard implication is **(B) + (I) ⟹ (D)**, where (I) is the Hodge standard / positivity conjecture and (D) is `hom = num`. (C) follows from (B) and is not what bridges hom to num. The page elsewhere correctly lists (I) but here drops it from the conjunction. Should read "(B) + (I)" (or directly "(D)").
- **motives.html:456 — "Q_ℓ-reps of Gal(Q̄/Q) … Tannakian, with G a quotient of G_Q itself (after ℓ-adic completion)."** Wrong direction: the Tannakian fundamental group of the category of finite-dim continuous `Q_ℓ`-representations is the **algebraic envelope** (Zariski closure / pro-algebraic completion) of the image of `G_Q`, not a quotient. For the full category it is essentially `G_Q` itself sitting inside its algebraic hull; a *single* faithful rep gives the Zariski closure of its image. "Quotient" inverts the natural map.
- **motives.html:425 — Crystalline row for Q(1).** Reads "`Q_p · 1/p`, with Frobenius φ acting by `1/p`." The underlying filtered φ-module of `Q_p(1)` has underlying space simply `Q_p`; the Frobenius eigenvalue is `1/p` (Hodge–Tate weight `−1`). Writing the underlying module as "`Q_p · 1/p`" is meaningless as a subspace of anything natural — it conflates the basis vector with a Frobenius eigenvalue label. The φ-action description is correct; the module description is muddled.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **§4 line 429 — "Hermite–Lindemann" for transcendence of `2πi`.** Transcendence of `π` is Lindemann (1882); the joint package "Hermite–Lindemann" usually refers to transcendence of `e^α` for algebraic `α ≠ 0`. Loose attribution but the conclusion is correct.
- **§6 line 525 — "étale realization `G_mot(Q) → Gal(Q̄/Q)`."** The precise relationship is a comparison morphism between the motivic Galois group and the algebraic envelope of `G_Q`'s image on `ℓ`-adic realizations; depending on conventions one talks about geometric vs arithmetic motivic Galois groups, and the natural map can run in the other direction once everything is taken pro-algebraically. The arrow direction as drawn is a defensible choice but the surrounding prose elides the subtlety.
- **§7 line 568 — "Artin" attribution for Q_ℓ-Tate-comparison.** Artin's comparison theorem is for finite coefficients (étale ≅ singular on `X(C)`); the `Q_ℓ` version is the inverse-limit / tensor-up corollary. Crediting "Artin" alone is loose shorthand; "Artin–Grothendieck" or "Artin comparison + ℓ-adic limit" would be more precise.
- **§5 line 455 — "Mumford–Tate group" for the Tannakian group of all Q-Hodge structures.** Strictly the Tannakian group of the whole category is the *universal* Mumford–Tate group; per object there is an MT group attached. The shorthand is standard but glosses the universal-vs-per-object distinction.
- **No motivic-cohomology computation, no `H(P^n) = ⊕ Q(-i)` worked.** The user asked specifically for the motivic decomposition `h(P^n) = ⊕_{i=0}^n Q(-i)`. Page only does the `n=1` case. Higher `P^n` is absent rather than wrong; a worked-out projector decomposition would close the gap. Similarly no `H^*_M(X, Q(n))` motivic-cohomology computation is shown anywhere on the page (the symbol does not appear).
