# Morse theory — math-correctness audit (2026-05)

Scope: every mathematical claim in `morse-theory.html` (1,284 lines, 9 sections + 7 widgets).

## Verified claims (sections)

- **§1 Morse functions.** Critical-point definition ($df_p=0$), well-definedness of the Hessian as a symmetric bilinear form on $T_pM$ at a critical point (chart-independence because $df_p=0$), Morse index = #neg eigenvalues. **Morse lemma** normal form $f = f(p) - x_1^2 - \cdots - x_k^2 + x_{k+1}^2 + \cdots + x_n^2$ correct. Isolation argument and Sard-style density of Morse functions in $C^\infty(M,\mathbb{R})$ correct (open + dense for compact $M$). Widget: $T^2$ height function $c_0,c_1,c_2 = (1,2,1)$, $\chi = 0$ correct.
- **§2 Handle decomposition.** Theorem A (regular intervals → diffeomorphism via $-\nabla f / \|\nabla f\|^2$) and Theorem B ($M^{c+\epsilon} \cong M^{c-\epsilon} \cup_\varphi (D^k \times D^{n-k})$, glued along $\partial D^k \times D^{n-k}$) correct. Sublevel widget for upright torus crossing min → saddle → saddle → max correct.
- **§3 CW structure.** Milnor's CW theorem (homotopy-equivalent to a CW complex with one $k$-cell per index-$k$ crit) correct. $\chi(M) = \sum (-1)^k c_k(f)$ correct and independent of $f$ via homological invariance.
- **§4 Morse inequalities.** Weak ($b_k \le c_k$) correct. Strong ($\sum_{k\le j}(-1)^{j-k}(c_k - b_k) \ge 0$) correct. Widget on $\Sigma_g$: $b = c = (1, 2g, 1)$ — standard height function on $\Sigma_g$ embedded as a "pretzel" achieves equality, so it is perfect; $\chi = 2-2g$ falls out. Existence of perfect Morse functions on $\mathbb{C}P^n$, flag varieties, compact Lie groups (Bott) correct.
- **§5 Morse homology.** Morse–Smale chain complex $C_k = \mathbb{Z}\langle\mathrm{Crit}_k\rangle$, boundary $\partial p = \sum \#\mathcal{M}(p,q)\cdot q$ over index-$(k-1)$ targets, $\partial^2 = 0$, and $HM_*(M) \cong H_*(M;\mathbb{Z})$ all correct. Widget: $T^2$ Morse complex $\mathbb{Z} \xleftarrow{0} \mathbb{Z}^2 \xleftarrow{0} \mathbb{Z}$ correct (each saddle has two flow lines to the unique min and they cancel with appropriate orientations / vanish mod 2).
- **§6 Reeb's theorem and $S^2$ vs $\mathbb{R}P^2$.** Reeb (2-crit ⇒ homeomorphic to $S^n$) correct; Milnor's exotic $S^7$ history correct. $\mathbb{R}P^2$ has $c=(1,1,1)$, $\chi=1$; the $\mathbb{Z}/2$ Betti number argument ($b_1(\mathbb{R}P^2;\mathbb{Z}/2)=1$ forces $c_1 \ge 1$ via strong inequalities mod 2) correct.
- **§7 Morse–Smale.** $W^s,W^u$ definitions (with $\Phi_t$ negative gradient flow), dimensions $n - \ind(p)$ and $\ind(p)$ correct, partition of $M$ correct, Morse–Smale transversality $T_x W^u(p) + T_x W^s(q) = T_x M$ correct. Smale's genericity (open dense set of metrics) correct. $\dim \mathcal{M}(p,q) = \ind(p) - \ind(q)$, $\dim \mathcal{M}(p,q)/\mathbb{R} = \ind(p) - \ind(q) - 1$ correct. The $\partial^2 = 0$ explanation via boundary of compactified 1-dim moduli = broken trajectories correct.
- **§8 Cerf theory.** Birth–death normal form $f = x_1^3 + Q(x_2,\dots,x_n) + \text{const}$ correct; perturbation $f_t = x_1^3 - t x_1 + Q$ produces consecutive-index pair correct. Widget arithmetic: $f_t(x) = x^3 - tx$, critical points at $x = \pm\sqrt{t/3}$ for $t>0$ ($f''(x)=6x$, so $+\sqrt{t/3}$ is min/index 0, $-\sqrt{t/3}$ is max/index 1 on $\mathbb{R}$) correct; critical values $\mp(2t/3)\sqrt{t/3}$ correct (verified algebraically); cusp at $t=0$ correct. $h$-cobordism statement (simply-conn cobordism, $H_*(W,M_0)=0$, $\dim M_i \ge 5$ ⇒ trivial) correct.

## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`morse-theory.html:285` — widget caption "two minima and maxima at the top/bottom rims (indices 0,2)".** Wording suggests two minima AND two maxima (4 extrema), but the upright torus has exactly ONE minimum (bottom rim) and ONE maximum (top rim) plus two saddles. The widget code at lines 651-655 correctly draws 1 min + 1 max + 2 saddles. Suggested fix: "minimum and maximum at the bottom/top rims".
- **`morse-theory.html:400` — equality at $j=n$ attribution "(Morse–Reeb / Lefschetz: the alternating sum is $\chi(M)$)".** This identity ($\sum (-1)^k c_k = \sum (-1)^k b_k = \chi$) is the **Euler–Poincaré** identity. "Morse–Reeb" is non-standard (Reeb's name attaches to the sphere theorem, not this). "Lefschetz" is misleading too (Lefschetz fixed-point formula is a different statement). Recommend "(Euler–Poincaré: the alternating sum equals $\chi(M)$)".
- **`morse-theory.html:1106` — Morse–Smale widget readout "saddle–saddle: $\langle\partial p, q\rangle = +1 + (-1) = 0$ over ℤ".** Calling this "$\langle\partial p,q\rangle$" is a category error. By definition, the Morse boundary $\partial$ only counts trajectories when $\ind(p) - \ind(q) = 1$. Two saddles of the same index ($\ind(p) = \ind(q) = 1$) contribute to $\partial^2$, not to $\partial$ — the $W^u(p) \cap W^s(q)$ count here is the (signed) intersection number whose pairwise cancellation is exactly what makes $\partial^2 = 0$ at that target. Suggested fix: relabel as "$\#(W^u(p) \cap W^s(q)) = +1 + (-1) = 0$" or as the contribution to $\langle\partial^2 p', q\rangle$ via the broken trajectory $p' \to p \to q$.
- **`morse-theory.html:585` — "Σ-cobordism, exotic ℝ⁴" parenthetical.** "Σ-cobordism" is non-standard / appears to be a typo. The standard term is **s-cobordism** (the non-simply-connected refinement). Even then, the right thing to cite for dim-4 failure is "smooth $h$-cobordism fails (Donaldson, exotic $\mathbb{R}^4$); topological $h$-cobordism survives (Freedman)". Recommend: drop "Σ-cobordism" or replace with "smooth $h$-cobordism fails in dimension 4".
- **`morse-theory.html:586` — "Hatcher–Wagoner theorem on $\pi_0\,\mathrm{Diff}(M^n)$".** Hatcher–Wagoner computes $\pi_0$ of the **pseudo-isotopy space** $\mathcal{P}(M)$ via $\mathrm{Wh}_2$ and $K_2(\mathbb{Z}[\pi_1])$; it relates to $\pi_0\,\mathrm{Diff}(M)$ only indirectly through pseudo-isotopy fibrations. As written, the statement misidentifies the target. Suggested fix: "$\pi_0$ of the pseudo-isotopy space $\mathcal{P}(M)$ for $\dim M$ large".

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`morse-theory.html:443` — "$HM_*(M) \cong H_*(M;\mathbb{Z})$".** The integer-coefficient version requires coherent orientations of moduli spaces (e.g. via a stable framing of $\det TM$); over $\mathbb{Z}/2$ no orientation choice is needed. The page does not flag the orientability hypothesis but the statement is standard for closed oriented manifolds. Soft under-spec, no error.
- **`morse-theory.html:291` — "Morse functions are *open and dense* in $C^\infty(M,\mathbb{R})$".** Strictly: in the Whitney $C^\infty$ topology, **open and dense for compact $M$**; on non-compact $M$ openness can fail. Section 2 introduces the closed-manifold assumption only later. Mild under-spec, harmless.
- **Witten deformation — absent.** The page does not mention Witten's analytic proof of Morse inequalities via the deformed Laplacian $d_t = e^{-tf} d e^{tf}$ and its small-eigenvalue asymptotics. Listed in the audit focus but not present in the prose.
- **Lusternik–Schnirelmann category vs Morse number — absent.** No mention of $\mathrm{cat}(M) \le \mathrm{Morse}(M)$ or the cup-length lower bound; Reeb's theorem is the only "minimum-critical-point" statement on the page. Listed in the audit focus but not present.
- **General height function on $S^n$, $T^n$ — implicit only.** Reeb covers the 2-critical-point case for $S^n$; $T^2$ is the only torus example. The full $T^n$ pattern ($\binom{n}{k}$ index-$k$ crits, perfect Morse function) is not stated. Not an error, just a gap relative to the audit focus.

## Severity

**Minor.** Five wording/attribution issues — a torus-widget caption miscount ("two minima and maxima"), the Euler–Poincaré identity misattributed to "Morse–Reeb / Lefschetz", a saddle–saddle widget readout that misuses $\langle\partial p, q\rangle$ for an index-difference-zero pair, the "Σ-cobordism" non-standard term, and a Hatcher–Wagoner target slip ($\pi_0\,\mathrm{Diff}$ should be $\pi_0\mathcal{P}$). All core mathematics is correct: Morse lemma, both inequality forms, handle decomposition, CW theorem, Morse homology = singular homology, Cerf birth-death normal form, and the $f_t = x^3 - tx$ widget arithmetic all check out. Three audit-focus topics (Witten deformation, LS category, general $S^n/T^n$ examples) are absent rather than wrong.
