## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **k-theory.html:263** — "the failure is genuine on $S^7$, for instance." Cancellation-failure for $\oplus$ on $S^7$ is false as stated: $S^7$ is parallelizable (one of the $S^1, S^3, S^7$ trio), $\widetilde K^0(S^7)=0$, and on a 7-complex stable bundles of rank $\ge 4$ are determined by their stable class, so cancellation actually holds for the standard examples on $S^7$. The textbook example is $TS^2$ on $S^2$ (with $TS^2\oplus\varepsilon\cong\varepsilon^3$ but $TS^2\not\cong\varepsilon^2$), or $TS^n$ for any $n\ne 1,3,7$. Replace "on $S^7$" with "on $S^2$" (or "on $S^4$").
