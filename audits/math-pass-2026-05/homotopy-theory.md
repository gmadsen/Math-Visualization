## Wrong / dubious claims

**`homotopy-theory.html:584` — Bott statement is off by a Z factor (twice).**
"Ω²U ≃ U×Z, Ω⁸O ≃ O×Z." Standard Bott: ΩU ≃ Z×BU and Ω(Z×BU) ≃ U, so **Ω²U ≃ U** (not U×Z); analogously **Ω⁸O ≃ O** (not O×Z). With "U×Z" you'd get π_0(Ω²U) = Z, but π_2(U) = 0. The "×Z" form holds for `Z×BU`, not `U`. Either drop the `×Z` factors, or restate as `Ω(Z×BU) ≃ U` and `Ω⁸(Z×BO) ≃ Z×BO`.

**`homotopy-theory.html:592` — π_n(U) row swapped.**
Table gives "Z, 0" under columns "n mod 2 = 0, 1". But π_0(U)=0 (U is connected) and π_1(U)=Z; pattern is **0, Z**, not Z, 0. (Equivalent fix: relabel columns so n=1 comes first.)

**`homotopy-theory.html:408` — K(Z/m,1) cohomology row.**
"Z[u]/(mu), |u|=2 (with Z/m on H^1)." With **integer** coefficients, H^1(K(Z/m,1);Z) = Hom(Z/m,Z) = 0 — the Z/m sits on H^2 (via Ext). The Z/m generator is on H^1 only with **Z/m** coefficients. Either delete the parenthetical, or specify "(with Z/m on H^1 with mod-m coefficients)."

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`:410`** "K(Z,n) for n≥3 … generated by Steenrod operations of ι_n." True for mod-p cohomology (Serre/Cartan); over Z it's looser. Worth qualifying as "mod-p" since the surrounding Steenrod-algebra paragraph (line 422) is mod-2.
- **`:422`** "Steenrod algebra A_2 = H*(K(Z/2,n);Z/2)-stable." The notation "-stable" is informal; standard statement is A_2 = colim_n H*(K(Z/2,n);Z/2) under suspension (or equivalently the algebra of stable mod-2 ops). Mathematically correct, just terse.
- **§4 line 472** sphere-spectrum structure map "Σ S^n = S^{n+1} ──=── S^{n+1}" — correct identification but the suspension-isomorphism convention should be the smash with S^1.
