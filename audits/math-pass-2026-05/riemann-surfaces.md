# Math-correctness audit: `riemann-surfaces.html`

## Verified claims

- **§1 Definition.** Atlas with biholomorphic transitions; orientability; equivalence of compact RS with smooth projective curves over $\mathbb{C}$; invariant table ($\chi=2-2g$, $\dim H^0(\Omega^1)=g$, $\deg K=2g-2$, $\dim\mathcal{M}_g=3g-3$ complex for $g\ge 2$). All standard and correct.
- **§2 Sphere.** Two-chart construction with transition $w=1/z$; stereographic formulas $X=2u/(1+|z|^2)$, $Z=(|z|^2-1)/(1+|z|^2)$ are right (north pole at $\infty$).
- **§2 Torus.** Lattice/$\tau$ formulation; $\tau=\omega_2/\omega_1$; $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ Möbius action; moduli $\mathbb{H}/\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$. Weierstrass $\wp$ giving the embedding into $y^2=4x^3-g_2x-g_3$. Standard fundamental-domain check $|\tau|\ge 1$, $|\mathrm{Re}\,\tau|\le 1/2$ (line 576).
- **§3 Branched cover monodromy.** Free generation of $\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^1\setminus B)$ mod the relation $\prod\gamma_i=1$; for double covers $\rho(\gamma_i)=\sigma$ forces $r$ even (line 824). Correct.
- **§4 Riemann–Hurwitz.** Formula $2g_X-2=n(2g_Y-2)+\sum(e_p-1)$ (line 851) and the double-cover specialisation $g_X=b/2-1$ (line 858). Both correct, including the calculator's `gx = 1 + n(gy-1) + halfRam` (line 908).
- **§5 Uniformization.** Trichotomy $\mathbb{CP}^1/\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{H}$; Fuchsian deck group in $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{R})$; $4g$-gon side identification $\prod[a_i,b_i]$; Gauss–Bonnet $\int\kappa\,dA=2\pi(2-2g)$. Correct.
- **§6 Riemann–Roch.** Statement $\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D-g+1$ with $K=$ canonical (line 1220). Genus-0 worked case $\ell(n\cdot\infty)=n+1$ (line 1227). Genus-1 table rows $\ell(2O)=2$ basis $\{1,\wp\}$, $\ell(3O)=3$ basis $\{1,\wp,\wp'\}$ (lines 1240–1242). Correct.
- **§7 Connections.** Serre duality, $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}=\mathbb{H}/\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, Teichmüller $\mathcal{T}_g\cong\mathbb{R}^{6g-6}$. Correct.

## Wrong / dubious claims

- **Line 277 — "countably many" structures.** "There are only countably many Riemann-surface structures on a surface of genus $g\ge 2$ … a $(6g-6)$-real-dimensional moduli space." A $(6g-6)$-real-dimensional moduli space has uncountably many points; the "countably many" is false (likely meant "a finite-dimensional family" or a swipe at the rigidity statement that the topology determines the dim). Self-contradictory as written.
- **Line 564 — `τ -> -τ` comment.** Code negates $\tau$ when $\mathrm{Im}\,\tau<0$, with comment "swap basis." Swapping $\omega_1\leftrightarrow\omega_2$ sends $\tau\to 1/\tau$, **not** $\tau\to-\tau$. Both happen to land in $\{\mathrm{Im}>0\}$, so the visualization is OK; but $\tau\to-\tau$ is not in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ (det $=1$ requires $\det\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=1$ which is true, but this maps to the same point of moduli only because $-I$ acts trivially — fine — but it is *not* the basis swap claimed). Comment is mathematically wrong.
- **Line 821 — genus formula `⌈(d−1)/2⌉`.** For $w^2=P(z)$, $\deg P=d$ squarefree, the correct hyperelliptic genus is $\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor$, not $\lceil\cdot\rceil$. Check: $d=2\Rightarrow g=0$ (sphere); ceiling gives $1$. $d=4\Rightarrow g=1$; ceiling gives $2$. The accompanying enumeration "$d=2$ gives a sphere, $d=3,4$ give a torus, $d=5,6$ give genus 2" *is* correct — so the symbol `⌈⌉` contradicts the prose immediately following it. Should be `⌊(d−1)/2⌋`.
- **Line 893 — `ellcover` example.** `{n:2, gy:1, eps:''}` with note "unramified double cover of a torus → genus 2 surface (this is how bielliptic curves arise)". Riemann–Hurwitz gives $g_X=1+2(1-1)+0=1$, not $2$. An *unramified* 2:1 cover of a torus is a torus. Bielliptic genus-2 curves require $r=2$ branch points (RH: $2(2)-2=0+r$). The example label is mathematically wrong; the calculator will display `g(X) = 1`, contradicting the note text.
- **Line 1245 — Abel/principal-divisor parenthetical.** "$1\cdot O - 0$ is zero in $\mathrm{Pic}^0$ iff $O = O$, trivially, but not in the sense of 'arising from a function'." $1\cdot O$ has degree $1$, so it isn't in $\mathrm{Pic}^0$ at all — the comparison is type-incorrect. The intended Abel statement is that $P-O$ (degree 0) is principal iff $P=O$. As written the sentence is muddled and effectively false.
- **Line 1245 — "$1\cdot\infty$ is the divisor of $1/z$".** $(1/z)$ on $\mathbb{CP}^1$ has divisor $\infty-0$ (zero at $\infty$, pole at $0$), not $1\cdot\infty$. Also $1/z\notin L(1\cdot\infty)$ since it has a pole at $0$. The valid statement would be $L(1\cdot\infty)=\langle 1, z\rangle$ giving $\ell(\infty)=2$, which the line then asserts — the answer is right but the supporting sentence is wrong.
- **Line 1115 — principal-divisor phrasing.** "the subgroup of principal divisors … is the kernel of the degree (on a compact $X$) on the level of equivalence classes." Principal divisors are *contained in* but not *equal to* $\ker\deg$; their quotient is $\mathrm{Pic}^0$, not the trivial group. Phrasing reads as if principal $=$ degree-zero, which would collapse $\mathrm{Pic}^0$.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **Line 895 — `exotic` example.** `{n:4, gy:1, eps:'3,3,2'}` gives $\sum(e_p-1)=5$ (odd), forcing $g_X=3.5$. The widget flags this with the warning path; if intentional pedagogy ("see what happens when you violate parity"), the note "exotic mixed ramification on a torus base" should say so. As-is it reads as a valid configuration.
- **Line 648 — "$X$ open in $\mathbb{C}$ iff simply connected and non-compact."** True for *Riemann surfaces*: simply-connected non-compact $X$ is biholomorphic to $\mathbb{C}$ or $\mathbb{H}\subset\mathbb{C}$. Statement holds but skips the Riemann mapping step; readers may parse it as a Liouville-style triviality.
- **Line 1284 — "meromorphic sections of powers of the Hodge bundle over $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ *are* modular forms."** Holomorphic sections of $\omega^{\otimes k}$ over the compactified $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{1,1}$ give holomorphic modular forms of weight $k$; meromorphic gives meromorphic forms. The shortcut is fine for §7 ("Connections") but elides the compactification subtlety.

## Severity

**Moderate.** The high-level theorem statements (RS definition, RH formula, RR statement, uniformization trichotomy, Gauss–Bonnet, hyperelliptic-cover monodromy) are all clean. The errors cluster in: (a) one wrong floor/ceiling (`⌈(d−1)/2⌉` → `⌊(d−1)/2⌋`, line 821) that contradicts its own enumeration, (b) one wrong calculator example (`ellcover` claims genus 2 but computes genus 1, line 893–894) that the user will see contradict itself live, (c) the "countably many structures" line, (d) two muddled sentences about principal divisors / $1/z$ in §6 that misuse degree typing, and (e) a comment-vs-action mismatch in the torus widget ($\tau\to-\tau$ is not basis-swap). None invalidate a section's main story; all are surgical fixes.
