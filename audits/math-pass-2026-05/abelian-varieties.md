## Wrong / dubious claims

- **abelian-varieties.html:714** — "$10$ reduction types: $I_0$ (good), $I_n$ (n≥1, multiplicative), and **seven** additive types $II,III,IV,I_0^*,I_n^*,IV^*,III^*,II^*$". The list of additive types contains **eight** entries, not seven. Standard count: 10 Kodaira families = $I_0$ + $I_n$ + 8 additive ($II,III,IV,I_0^*,I_n^*,II^*,III^*,IV^*$). Either change "seven" → "eight" or drop one entry (which would break the count).
- **abelian-varieties.html:528–529 (widget bound)** — "Weil: $|N - (q^n+1)^g| \le \binom{2g}{g}\,q^{ng/2}$". Not the standard Weil/Lang–Weil bound for AVs. From $\#A(\mathbb F_q)=\prod(1-\alpha_i)$ and the symplectic pairing $\alpha_i\alpha_{i+g}=q$, the natural "main term" is $q^g$ (constant term of $P_A(T)$ at $T=1$), not $(q+1)^g$. The bound $|N-(q+1)^g|\le((1+\sqrt q)^{2g}-(q+1)^g)$ holds via $|1-\alpha_i|\le1+\sqrt q$, but $\binom{2g}{g}q^{ng/2}$ is not the textbook constant. Quote-as-Weil is misleading; cite as a heuristic order-of-magnitude or restate.
- **abelian-varieties.html:603 (widget table row $g=2$)** — "every p.p.a.v. is a Jacobian" with hover text qualifying "or, in degenerate cases, a product of elliptic curves with the product polarization." A product of two ECs with the product polarization is not a Jacobian of a smooth genus-2 curve — it's a degenerate (decomposable) p.p.a.v. The row text should say "every irreducible p.p.a.v. is a Jacobian" to match row 3's phrasing for $g=3$. Minor wording inconsistency that mis-states the dichotomy in dim 2.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **abelian-varieties.html:114 (Tate-module quiz, choice C)** — "Weil pairing gives a Galois-equivariant alternating pairing $T_\ell A\times T_\ell A\to\mathbb Z_\ell(1)$" marked correct. Without a polarization the Weil pairing is $T_\ell A\times T_\ell A^\vee\to\mathbb Z_\ell(1)$; only after fixing a (principal) polarization does it descend to $T_\ell A\times T_\ell A\to\mathbb Z_\ell(1)$. The question premise doesn't fix a polarization. Standard textbook compression — flag as imprecise rather than wrong.
- **abelian-varieties.html:268 (cube relation).** The displayed alternating tensor expression is correct as a special case of the theorem of the cube applied to the three projections $A^3\to A$, but the theorem itself is the more general statement about $f+g+h:T\to A$ for arbitrary maps from a connected scheme. The page presents it as the whole content of the theorem. Minor compression.
- **abelian-varieties.html:592–595** — "$\dim\mathcal M_g=3g-3$ for $g\ge2$". Correct for $g\ge2$; $\dim\mathcal M_1=1$ is implicitly given in the table row. Note $\mathcal M_0$ and $\mathcal M_1$ have different formulas ($0$ and $1$ respectively); the formula $3g-3$ is restricted properly. No issue.
- **abelian-varieties.html:790 (widget readout `g0`/`av-points`)** — "$A$ doesn't exist in dim $0$ (it would be a point)." A 0-dim AV is the point $\{e\}$, which is a perfectly valid (trivial) abelian variety. Pedagogical handwave; not a bug.
