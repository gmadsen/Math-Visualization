## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

**simplicial-complexes-combinatorial.html:422** — "Stanley's g-theorem (1980, with McMullen's g-conjecture from 1971 as the upper-bound side)". McMullen's g-conjecture is not the "upper-bound side"; it is the **full characterization** of f-vectors of simplicial polytopes (the upper-bound theorem is a separate, weaker result — McMullen 1970 for polytopes, Stanley 1975 for spheres). The g-theorem name covers both Stanley 1980 (sufficiency, via toric varieties) and Billera–Lee 1980 (necessity). Adiprasito 2018 extended g-conjecture to all simplicial spheres — worth a mention since the page is dated 2026.
