## Wrong / dubious claims

- `intersection-theory-chow.html:628` — "Quillen's affine bundle lemma $A_*(\mathbb{A}^n)=\mathbb{Z}$" — misattribution. The Chow homotopy property $A_*(X\times\mathbb{A}^n)\cong A_{*-n}(X)$ is in Fulton (Prop 1.9) and is classical (Bloch / etc.); Quillen's "homotopy invariance" is the K-theory statement $K_i(R)\cong K_i(R[t])$ for regular $R$. The two are analogous, but ascribing the Chow statement to Quillen is incorrect.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- §2 (`:340`) "Griffiths' results on the Fano threefold" — Griffiths' classical example of homologically-trivial-but-not-algebraically-trivial cycles is on the **cubic threefold** (a Fano threefold, but the specific reference is "the Fano variety of lines on the cubic threefold" or the cubic threefold itself for $\mathrm{CH}_1$). Calling it just "the Fano threefold" is loose; not wrong but readers may struggle to find the reference.
- §3 (`:483`) "On singular $X$ the moving lemma fails in general — one has to descend to operational Chow groups or work with the bivariant theory of Fulton–MacPherson" — correct in substance, but "descend to operational Chow groups" is a slight misstatement: operational Chow $A^*_{\mathrm{op}}(X)$ is a *substitute* for the missing ring structure on singular $X$, not a "descent." Minor wording quibble.
- §5 widget readout (`:809`) "the top non-zero Chern class is in codimension at most ${rank}$" — true and tight (top Chern is always in degree exactly $r$ generically, but can vanish). Phrasing is fine.
- The `'E'` (elliptic curve) widget scenario in §2 uses a "$\mathbb{Z}/3$ toy" key for visual demo (`:386`); this is openly disclosed in the readout.
