## Wrong / dubious claims

- **L1227 (functoriality widget, base-change row).** "$(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n) \mapsto (\alpha_1^{[E:\mathbb{Q}]}, \dots, \alpha_n^{[E:\mathbb{Q}]})$ at split primes." This is **backwards**: at primes that split completely in $E/\mathbb{Q}$, the base-change Satake parameters at each place above $p$ are *unchanged* (still $(\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n)$). The $[E:\mathbb{Q}]$-th-power formula applies at **inert** primes (where the residue degree equals $[E:\mathbb{Q}]$, so Frobenius acts as $\mathrm{Frob}_p^{[E:\mathbb{Q}]}$). Should read "at inert primes" (or, generally, "at unramified primes, raise to the residue-degree power").
- **L986 (residual spectrum, GL_2/Q).** "A one-parameter family of one-dimensional representations $g \mapsto |\det g|^s$ at $s = 1/2$." Misleading. The residual spectrum of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A})^1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is **not** a one-parameter family — it consists of one-dimensional automorphic characters $g \mapsto \chi(\det g)$ where $\chi$ is a Hecke character of $\mathbb{A}^\times/\mathbb{Q}^\times$ of finite order (and the residue arises from $E(g,s)$ at $s=1$ in the classical variable / $s=1/2$ in the unitary). The phrase "one-parameter family ... at $s = 1/2$" conflates the location of the pole (a single point) with a continuous family. It is a **discrete** set indexed by Hecke characters $\chi$ with $\chi^2 = 1$ on $\mathbb{A}^\times/\mathbb{Q}^\times \mathbb{R}_{>0}$.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **L793.** "$\dim \pi_p^{K_0(p^{c_p+j})} = j + 1$" stated for any "irreducible admissible $\pi_p$". Casselman's formula requires $\pi_p$ to be **generic** (equivalently infinite-dimensional for $\mathrm{GL}_2$) — one-dimensional $\pi_p$ are an exception. Minor: should add the genericity caveat.
- **L871.** "At ramified primes ... $(1 - a_p p^{-s})^{-1}$ with $a_p$ a single Hecke eigenvalue or, in worse cases, $1$." Correct by case (special / supercuspidal / ramified principal series), but "$a_p$ a single Hecke eigenvalue" elides that for ramified primes this is no longer the classical Hecke $T_p$ eigenvalue (it's an Atkin–Lehner / unramified-twist datum). Acceptable simplification but worth flagging.
- **L1188.** "Sato–Tate ... established by Newton–Thorne (2021)." Slight misattribution: Newton–Thorne (2021) proved $\mathrm{Sym}^n$-functoriality for all $n \geq 1$ (non-CM holomorphic forms over $\mathbb{Q}$); the original Sato–Tate for non-CM elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$ was Clozel–Harris–Shepherd-Barron–Taylor (2008–10). Newton–Thorne removes earlier residue-characteristic restrictions and extends the symmetric power range. Minor wording.
- **L875–876 vs L869–870.** Note silently switches Satake normalisation ("unitary" $\alpha_p$ on the unit circle vs. "classical" $\alpha_p\beta_p = p^{k-1}$). Mathematically consistent (the rescaling $\alpha \mapsto p^{(k-1)/2}\alpha$ converts), but the normalisation switch isn't flagged for the reader.
