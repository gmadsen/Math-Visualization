## Verified claims (sections)

- **§1 Knots and links.** Knot = embedding $S^1 \hookrightarrow S^3$ mod ambient isotopy; $S^3$ vs $\mathbb{R}^3$ rationale (one-point compactification, isotopy can avoid ∞); 4-valent diagram; crossing number $c(3_1) = 3$, $c(4_1) = 4$ ✓.
- **§2 Reidemeister moves.** R1 changes writhe by ±1; R2 / R3 preserve writhe ✓. R1↔ribbon-twist, R2↔$RR^{-1}=\mathrm{id}$, R3↔Yang–Baxter $R_{12}R_{13}R_{23}=R_{23}R_{13}R_{12}$ ✓.
- **§3 Alexander.** $\Delta_K(t)=\det(V-tV^T)$ ∈ $\mathbb{Z}[t,t^{-1}]$, well-defined up to $\pm t^k$ ✓. Symmetry $\Delta(t^{-1})=\Delta(t)$ up to units ✓. Conway-normalized skein $\Delta(L_+)-\Delta(L_-)=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})\Delta(L_0)$ ✓. $\Delta_{K^\ast}=\Delta_K$ (mirror invariance) ✓. Conway / Kinoshita–Terasaka have $\Delta=1$ ✓. Catalog values: $\Delta_{3_1}=t-1+t^{-1}$, $\Delta_{4_1}=-t+3-t^{-1}$, $\Delta_{T(5,2)}=t^2-t+1-t^{-1}+t^{-2}$, $\Delta_{5_2}=2t-3+2t^{-1}$, all ✓ against standard tables. $|\Delta_{4_1}(-1)|=5$ ✓ (det of $4_1$). Genus of $T(5,2) = (5-1)(2-1)/2 = 2$ ✓.
- **§4 Jones (statements).** Bracket axioms (skein, loop $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$, empty=1) ✓. R1 multiplies bracket by $-A^{\pm 3}$ ✓. $X(D)=(-A)^{-3w(D)}\langle D\rangle$ is invariant ✓; $A=q^{-1/4}$ converts to $V_K(q)$ ✓. $V_{3_1}=-q^{-4}+q^{-3}+q^{-1}$ for right trefoil; mirror has $V=-q^4+q^3+q$ — chirality ✓. Skein $q^{-1}V(L_+)-q\,V(L_-)=(q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2})V(L_0)$ ✓.
- **§5 HOMFLY (axioms).** Skein $\alpha P(L_+)-\alpha^{-1}P(L_-)=z\,P(L_0)$, $P(\bigcirc)=1$ ✓. Specialisations: $\alpha=1, z=t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2}\Rightarrow\Delta$ ✓; $\alpha=q^{-1}, z=q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2}\Rightarrow V$ ✓. $P(\bigcirc\sqcup L) = \frac{\alpha-\alpha^{-1}}{z}P(L)$ ✓.
- **§6 Quantum.** $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ standard rep → Jones; $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_n)$ → HOMFLY specialization; $\mathrm{Sym}^N$ → coloured Jones; $U_q(\mathfrak{so}_n)$ → Kauffman ✓. Volume conjecture statement ✓.
- **§7 Vassiliev.** Singular-knot rule $V(K_\times)=V(K_+)-V(K_-)$ ✓. Order-$\le n$ filtration, $\mathcal{V}_n/\mathcal{V}_{n-1}$ finite-dim via chord diagrams + 4T relation ✓. $V_K(e^h)=\sum v_n h^n$ with $v_n$ of order $\le n$ (Bar-Natan) ✓. Dim table 1,0,1,1,3 ✓ against Bar-Natan.

## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`knot-polynomials.html:881–891` — Kauffman state-sum circle counts on the trefoil are wrong.** The widget asserts `AAA→2, AAB→3, ABA→3, BAA→3, ABB→2, BAB→2, BBA→2, BBB→1` circles. Plugging these into $\sum A^{a-b}\delta^{|s|-1}$ gives $2A^5+2A+A^{-3}$, NOT the announced $\langle 3_1\rangle=-A^5-A^{-3}+A^{-7}$. The standard counts for the right-handed trefoil are `AAA→2, AAB=ABA=BAA→1, ABB=BAB=BBA→2, BBB→3`; with those, $A^3\delta+3A+3A^{-1}\delta+A^{-3}\delta^2$ correctly collapses to $-A^5-A^{-3}+A^{-7}$. The widget's per-state numbers are A↔B swapped relative to the diagram convention; the stated final answer (line 911) is the standard right-trefoil bracket and IS correct, but the per-state arithmetic shown to the reader does not produce it. **Moderate** — the centerpiece worked example doesn't reconcile.
- **`knot-polynomials.html:944, 960` — HOMFLY of the trefoil widget is wrong.** The "fully reduced" stage prints $P(3_1)=-\alpha^{-2}+\alpha^2+\alpha^{-2}z^2$. Sanity check via the Alexander specialization ($\alpha=1, z^2=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})^2=t-2+t^{-1}$): the formula yields $-1+1+(t-2+t^{-1})=t-2+t^{-1}$, but $\Delta_{3_1}=t-1+t^{-1}$. The standard HOMFLY of the right trefoil under the page's skein convention is $P(3_1)=-\alpha^4+\alpha^2 z^2+2\alpha^2$ (or, after mirror/sign convention, $-\alpha^{-4}+\alpha^{-2}z^2+2\alpha^{-2}$). The intermediate "α² P(unknot) + α z P(Hopf)" stage on line 942 is also dimensionally off — the leading coefficient of the trefoil expansion is $\alpha^2$ only after normalising one strand to itself, but the constant offset is missing. **Moderate.**
- **`knot-polynomials.html:727` — "Hopf link complement is fibred over the circle by Seifert tori."** The Hopf link IS fibred, but the fibre is an **annulus**, not a torus. The complement $S^3\setminus(\bigcirc\sqcup\bigcirc)$ deformation retracts to $T^2$, but that is the homotopy type, not a fibration with torus fibres over $S^1$. As stated, the sentence conflates "fibre is a torus" with "complement contains a torus". **Minor wording bug.**
- **`knot-polynomials.html:1032, 1042` — R-matrix limit at $q=1$ is mis-described.** Code comment line 990 says "at q=1 it degenerates to the swap (classical)"; readout line 1042 says "At q = 1 only the diagonal survives and R = id". The matrix shown — $\mathrm{diag}(q,1,1,q)$ with $(q-q^{-1})$ at entry $(01,10)$ — at $q=1$ becomes the identity matrix, NOT the swap. The two lines contradict each other; identity is what the matrix actually does. The phrase "swap on swap-symmetric tensors" (line 1042) is also vacuous (every operator is the identity on its own fixed vectors). **Minor.**
- **`knot-polynomials.html:577–579` — Khovanov chain-group formula has $j$ on both sides.** $\widetilde{C}^{i,j}(D)=\bigoplus_{|s|-n_-=i}A^{\otimes c(s)}\{j-i+n_+-2n_-\}$ uses $\{j-i+n_+-2n_-\}$ as a grading shift but $j$ is also the grading index of $C^{i,j}$ — making the formula self-referential. Standard Khovanov: define $C^i:=\bigoplus_{|s|-n_-=i} A^{\otimes c(s)}\{i+n_+-2n_-\}$ (graded module, no $j$ on the RHS), then $C^{i,j}$ is the degree-$j$ piece. **Minor presentation bug.**
- **`knot-polynomials.html:327` — "trefoil has the braid group $B_3$ on 2 generators".** The trefoil knot group $\pi_1(S^3\setminus 3_1)\cong \langle a,b\mid aba=bab\rangle$ is isomorphic to $B_3$, but $B_3$ is the braid group on **3 strands** (which has 2 generators $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$). "$B_3$ on 2 generators" reads as if $B_3$ means "braids on 2 things", which would be $B_2\cong\mathbb{Z}$. Should read "the braid group $B_3$ on 3 strands (2 generators)". **Minor wording.**

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`knot-polynomials.html:945` — "δ = (α − α⁻¹)/z is the value of P(unknot ⊔ unknot)/P(unknot)".** This identity is correct as the multiplier when adjoining a free unknot, but writing it as a "δ" overloads the bracket-section's $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$. Different object, same letter — non-fatal but easy to misread.
- **`knot-polynomials.html:842` — "5_2 different from 5_1 — Alexander already distinguishes 5_1 and 5_2".** True; $\Delta_{5_1}\ne\Delta_{5_2}$ as Laurent polynomials. Worth flagging that they DO have the same determinant (|Δ(−1)| = 5 for both? actually $|\Delta_{5_1}(-1)|=5$ and $|\Delta_{5_2}(-1)|=7$, so no — they differ at $t=-1$ too).
- **§4 figure-eight as "amphichiral" widget readout (line 726).** True ($4_1$ is amphichiral); the related claim about "largest hyperbolic volume of any 4-crossing knot" is trivially true (it's the only hyperbolic 4-crossing knot — vol $\approx 2.0299$).

## Severity

**Moderate.** Two centerpiece worked examples have wrong arithmetic that is visible to any reader who tries to reproduce: (1) the trefoil Kauffman state-table circle counts don't sum to the announced bracket; (2) the trefoil HOMFLY widget's "fully reduced" formula fails the Alexander specialization sanity check. Both stated *final* answers (bracket $-A^5-A^{-3}+A^{-7}$, Jones $-q^{-4}+q^{-3}+q^{-1}$) and all general theorems are correct — the bug is in the worked-example bookkeeping. Plus a contradictory R-matrix limit (id vs swap), one minor wording on Hopf-link fibration, one self-referential Khovanov formula, and a "$B_3$ on 2 generators" gloss. Recommend fixing the two widget computations before any further use; the rest are one-line copy edits.
