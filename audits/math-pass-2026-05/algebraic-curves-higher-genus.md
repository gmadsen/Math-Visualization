**§1 Smooth projective curves and genus.** All four genus characterisations agree: $g = \dim H^0(C,\Omega^1) = h^1(\mathcal O_C) = 1 - \chi(\mathcal O_C) = 1 - \tfrac12\chi_{\rm top}$ (line 276). Numerology table (lines 281–290): $\chi_{\rm top}=2-2g$, $\deg K_C = 2g-2$, $\dim\mathcal M_g = 3g-3$ for $g \ge 2$ — all correct. Riemann–Hurwitz widget formula $g = b/2 - d + 1$ (line 371) is the correct specialisation to $g_{\mathbb P^1}=0$ with $b$ simple branch points, and the bound $b \ge 2d-2$ (line 397) is the right necessary condition.

**§2 Divisors.** Picard group decomposition $\mathrm{Pic}(C) = \bigsqcup \mathrm{Pic}^d$ with $\mathrm{Pic}^0 = \mathrm{Jac}$ a $g$-dim'l torus $\mathbb C^g/\Lambda$ over $\mathbb C$, period lattice from $H_1(C,\mathbb Z) \cong \mathbb Z^{2g}$, principal polarisation, Abel–Jacobi $\mathrm{Sym}^d C \to \mathrm{Pic}^d$ — all standard and correct. Volume claim $|\det\,\mathrm{Im}\,\tau|$ matches the Riemann period normalisation.

**§3 Riemann–Roch.** Statement $h^0(D) - h^0(K-D) = \deg D - g + 1$ (line 474), Serre duality identification $h^0(K-D) = h^1(D)$, vanishing for $\deg D > 2g-2$, and $D=K \Rightarrow h^0(K)=g$ all correct. The negative-degree vanishing argument (line 506) is correct: a section of $\mathcal O(K-D)$ would give a principal divisor of positive degree. Worked $g=2$, $D=3[p]$ example: $h^0(D)=2$, classifies as $g^1_3$, induces degree-3 map to $\mathbb P^1$. Verified that for generic $p$ the linear system is base-point-free ($h^0(2p)=1 \ne h^0(3p)=2$), so map degree is indeed 3. Weierstrass gap sequence claims (line 524): $g$ gaps in $[1,2g-1]$, generic $\{1,\ldots,g\}$, hyperelliptic Weierstrass $\{1,3,\ldots,2g-1\}$ — all correct.

**§4 Canonical embedding.** $|K|$ b.p.f. for $g\ge2$, $\dim |K| = g-1$. Embedding iff non-hyperelliptic, image of degree $2g-2$ in $\mathbb P^{g-1}$. Hyperelliptic factorisation through degree-$(g-1)$ rational normal curve. Genus 3 cases (smooth plane quartic vs. conic) correct. Genus 4: canonical curve = quadric ∩ cubic in $\mathbb P^3$, unique quadric — correct (the quadric is always unique; smooth iff two $g^1_3$'s, cone iff one).

**§5 Hyperelliptic curves.** $y^2 = f(x)$ with $f$ separable, $\deg f \in \{2g+1, 2g+2\}$, branch points = roots of $f$ (plus $\infty$ if $\deg f$ odd). Three characterisations (geometric / canonical / RR via $h^0(p+q)=2$) are equivalent. Codim of hyperelliptic locus = $g-2$. Total Weierstrass weight $g^3-g$; hyperelliptic case: $(2g+2) \cdot \binom{g}{2} = g^3-g$ — verified. Riemann–Hurwitz check in widget readout (line 728) computes $2g-2 = -4 + (2g+2)$ ✓.

**§6 Moduli.** $\mathcal M_g$ DM stack for $g\ge2$, dim $3g-3$, deformation count $h^1(T_C) = 3g-3$ (= $h^0(2K)$ by RR). Compactification $\overline{\mathcal M}_g$ by stable nodal curves; boundary divisors $\Delta_0, \ldots, \Delta_{\lfloor g/2\rfloor}$ with the body-text dimension $3g-4$ (line 909). Hodge class $\lambda$ + boundary classes generate $\mathrm{Pic}_\mathbb Q$ (Mumford).

## Wrong / dubious claims

**`algebraic-curves-higher-genus.html:882` — boundary-divisor dimension formula in widget readout.** The interactive readout displays
`dim Δ_i = (3i−3) + (3(g−i)−3) + 1 (the node) = 3g − 5 (codim 1 in Mg-bar)`.
The arithmetic gives $3g-5$, but the divisor has dimension $3g-4$ (codim 1 of $\dim \overline{\mathcal M}_g = 3g-3$), as the surrounding prose at line 909 correctly states. The error: each side contributes $\dim \overline{\mathcal M}_{i,1} = 3i-2$ (one marked point for the gluing node), not $\dim \overline{\mathcal M}_i = 3i-3$. Correct formula: $(3i-2) + (3(g-i)-2) = 3g-4$, no extra $+1$ for the node. The widget's "codim 1" verdict is right by accident (or by override from the body text); the displayed arithmetic contradicts it.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`:558` "Max Noether's theorem (or 'Enriques–Babbage')"** — conflates two distinct results. Max Noether: canonical map embeds iff non-hyperelliptic (and generates the canonical ring for non-hyp curves). Enriques–Babbage / Petri: canonical ideal is generated by quadrics for $g \ge 4$ non-hyp non-trigonal non-plane-quintic. The named-as-equivalent parenthetical is a mild misattribution.
- **`:564` "Petri's theorem in low genus"** for the genus-4 quadric-and-cubic statement is loose — the quadric/cubic intersection is more elementary (numerics + canonical-ring generation); Petri's theorem proper governs higher genus and quadric generation.
- **`:777` "$\mathrm{Teich}_g \to \mathcal M_g$ is its universal cover"** — Teichmüller space covers $\mathcal M_g$ as an orbifold, not in the strict topological sense (since $\mathcal M_g$ is not a manifold). Defensible shorthand but imprecise.
- **`:784` "$\mathcal M_{1,1} \cong \mathbb A^1_j$"** — true at the level of coarse moduli; as a stack $\mathcal M_{1,1}$ has $\mu_4, \mu_6$ stabilisers at $j=1728, 0$. Standard abuse of notation.
- **Faltings/Mordell** mentioned only as a name-drop in the outro (line 952); no specific claim to verify.
- **Brill–Noether** appears only in callbacks/links; the page itself has no Brill–Noether numerical claims to audit.
