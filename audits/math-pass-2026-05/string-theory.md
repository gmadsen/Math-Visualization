## Wrong / dubious claims

**`string-theory.html:1007` — "Type-IIB string theory on $\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$ … in the $\lambda\to\infty,\ N\to\infty$ limit."** The classical-supergravity limit of AdS/CFT is the planar 't Hooft limit $N\to\infty$ at fixed (large) $\lambda$, then $\lambda\to\infty$ separately. Conflating both into "$\lambda\to\infty, N\to\infty$" elides the order of limits and obscures the $1/N$ vs $1/\sqrt\lambda$ corrections. Minor wording, but the standard statement is "planar limit + strong-coupling limit," not a simultaneous double limit.

**`string-theory.html:1005` — "U(N) gauge group" for the boundary $\mathcal N=4$ SYM.** The strict AdS/CFT dictionary uses $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ (or $\mathrm{PSU}(N)$); the diagonal $U(1)$ on the D3-brane stack decouples (corresponds to a free centre-of-mass mode and is not part of the interacting CFT dual to bulk gravity). Common pedagogical shortcut, but technically inaccurate.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`:627–631` "massless content sketch" rows omit the NS-NS antisymmetric 2-form $B_{\mu\nu}$** for IIA, IIB, and heterotic — the $B$-field is part of the universal NS-NS sector (graviton + dilaton + $B_2$) and is one of the three massless bosonic degrees of freedom the audit prompt explicitly named. Type I correctly omits it (orientifold projection kills NS-NS $B$ but keeps RR $C_2$). The "sketch" caveat covers this, but for IIA/IIB/heterotic the omission is a real content gap, not just a level of detail.
- **`:627–631` fermion content unspecified** beyond "gauginos" for type I — gravitinos, dilatinos, and (for type II) the doubled gravitino multiplet are not listed despite being part of the audit prompt's "graviton, antisymmetric tensor, dilaton + fermions" target.
- **BPS states / black hole entropy match** (Strominger–Vafa 1996): not discussed anywhere on the page despite being one of string theory's most cited quantitative successes (microscopic D1-D5-P state count reproduces the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy of the corresponding extremal 5D black hole). The audit prompt asked about it; its absence is a content gap, not a wrong claim.
- **Closed-string level matching $N=\tilde N$** (or $N-\tilde N=nm$ with KK + winding) is never written down. The §6 mass formula uses $N+\tilde N$ but the level-matching constraint that pairs left- and right-movers — central to "specific worked examples (closed string spectrum, level matching)" in the audit prompt — is implicit.
- **`:635` "M-theory at strong coupling … 11-dimensional theory"**: stated correctly but with no further structure (no mention of Witten 1995, no IIA-at-strong-coupling ↔ M on $S^1$ or heterotic $E_8\times E_8$ ↔ M on $S^1/\mathbb Z_2$).
- **`:520` "graviton for the closed string" at "$N=1$"**: the open-string mass formula in §3 has $\alpha' M^2 = N-1$; for the closed string the analogous formula is $\alpha' M^2 = 4(N-1)=4(\tilde N-1)$ with level matching. Saying "the closed string graviton appears at $N=1$" is right but informal — the page never quotes the closed-string version of the mass formula in §3.
- **`:514` "Lorentz invariance … forces $a=1$"**: the standard derivation is closure of the $[J^{i-},J^{j-}]$ commutator in light-cone gauge, which simultaneously fixes $a=1$ AND $D=26$ (two conditions from one commutator). Page presents them as sequential.

## Severity

**Minor.** All quantitative claims are correct: action normalisations, central-charge arithmetic ($D=26$, $D=10$), CY Hodge data (quintic $(1,101)$, bicubic $(2,83)$, Schoen $(19,19)$, $\chi=2(h^{1,1}-h^{2,1})$), CDOGP curve counts, T-duality mass formula and self-dual radius, RR-form content per theory, Monster-order and McKay–Thompson coefficients, AdS/CFT setup. The two flagged dubious items (U(N) vs SU(N), the simultaneous-limit phrasing) are pedagogical shortcuts the field commonly tolerates. The real shortcomings are content gaps the audit prompt asked about: the NS-NS $B$-field is missing from the massless spectra, fermions beyond "gauginos" aren't itemised, BPS / black-hole entropy isn't covered, and explicit level matching never appears. None of this rises to a math error; it's underspecification.
