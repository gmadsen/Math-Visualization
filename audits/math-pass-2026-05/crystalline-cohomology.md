## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **crystalline-cohomology.html:535 — supersingular elliptic curve "no global W-lift".** Widget data row says `lift: 'no global W-lift in general (only W₂)'`. This is **flatly wrong** and **directly contradicts the prose in line 504** ("elliptic curves always lift to $W$ via Serre–Tate, though for supersingular $E_0/k$ the lift is non-canonical"). Every elliptic curve over a perfect field of char $p$ admits a smooth lift to $W(k)$; for supersingular $E_0$ the lift is merely non-canonical (no Serre–Tate canonical lift), but it exists. Fix: change to `'yes (non-canonical lift; no Serre–Tate canonical)'`.
- **crystalline-cohomology.html:553 — "the textbook case where global lifts fail".** Reads: *"Even on the supersingular elliptic curve — the textbook case where global lifts fail — crystalline cohomology gives a free rank-2 W-module."* Wrong premise: lifts for supersingular elliptic curves do **not** fail. The textbook examples of "global lifts fail" are Hirokado / Schröer surfaces or threefolds, or Enriques surfaces in char 2, not supersingular elliptic curves. Fix: rephrase to *"Even on the supersingular elliptic curve — where the canonical Serre–Tate lift does not exist — crystalline cohomology…"*. The downstream "the crystalline site does the gluing the lift cannot" sentence then also needs softening, since the lift exists; the right point is that the construction doesn't depend on a choice of lift.
- **crystalline-cohomology.html:777 — "Tsuji 1999" attribution for crystalline comparison.** Faltings 1989 is correct for the crystalline ($C_{\mathrm{cris}}$) conjecture in the good-reduction case. Tsuji 1999 proved the **semistable** ($C_{\mathrm{st}}$) conjecture, not crystalline. Crystalline-only proofs are Fontaine–Messing 1987 (low-dim), Faltings 1989 (general good reduction), and Niziol's K-theory proof. Fix: either drop "Tsuji 1999" or rebrand the box as "crystalline / semistable comparison" and keep both.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **§1 line 272 — "free of rank equal to the Betti number".** $H^i_{\mathrm{cris}}(X_0/W)$ is finitely generated but **not always torsion-free** (e.g., Enriques surfaces in char 2 carry 2-torsion). Stating it as flatly "free of rank $b_i$" is true after $\otimes K$ for smooth proper $X_0$, and integrally only under torsion-free hypotheses (no $p$-torsion on $H^*$ and $H^{*+1}$). Should be qualified, or restricted to $\otimes K$.
- **§2 line 362 — "$\gamma_n(p)=p^n/n!\in pW$ — divisible because…$v_p(p^n/n!)\ge 1$".** The valuation bound shows $\gamma_n(p)\in pW$, but **divisibility of $n!$ in $W$** is what the construction needs; the prose conflates the two. Substantively the standard PD on $pW$ exists, but the one-line justification is thin.
- **Audit-requested topics not present.** The page does **not** cover (a) Dwork's proof of rationality, (b) rigid cohomology / Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology, or (c) any concrete Frobenius-matrix computation on $H^1_{\mathrm{cris}}$ of $\mathbb{P}^n$ beyond the eigenvalue list. These are listed as audit foci but appear nowhere in the file — coverage gap, not wrong content.
- **§5 line 741 — "$p$-adic monodromy theorem".** Invoked without statement or attribution (Crew / André / Kedlaya / Mebkhout). Probably means the local monodromy theorem for $p$-adic differential equations; underspecified.
- **§6 — $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ construction.** Glossed as "perfection of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}_p}/p$, taking PD-envelopes, and inverting a specific element". The "specific element" is $t = \log[\varepsilon]$ (Fontaine's $t$, a $p$-adic $2\pi i$); not naming it leaves the construction opaque. Not wrong, just thin.

## Severity

**minor–moderate** — two genuine math errors and one attribution error. The supersingular-lift bug (line 535 + line 553) is the most worrying because it propagates a false statement through both an interactive widget readout and a section conclusion, and contradicts the page's own correct prose two paragraphs earlier. The Tsuji-1999 attribution is a small history mistake. Otherwise the algebraic core (PD axioms, crystalline-site definition, Berthelot–Ogus, Katz–Messing, Dieudonné–Manin, Mazur, Fontaine comparison) is sound and the worked examples (PD-valuation widget, $E/\mathbb{F}_3$ and $E/\mathbb{F}_5$ Frobenius char polys, ordinary-vs-supersingular Newton polygon) compute correctly.
