## Wrong / dubious claims (with file:line)

- **`surgery-theory.html:514` — $|bP_{4k}|$ formula off by factor at k=3.** The text gives $|bP_{4k}| = 2^{2k-2}(2^{2k-1}-1)\,\mathrm{numerator}(B_{2k}/4k)$. Spot-check: k=2 gives $4\cdot 7\cdot\mathrm{num}(-1/240)=28$ ✓; k=3 gives $16\cdot 31\cdot\mathrm{num}(1/504)=496$, but the actual $|bP_{12}|=992$ (which the widget at line 788 itself reports). The Kervaire–Milnor formula is $|bP_{4k}|=2^{2k-2}(2^{2k-1}-1)\,\mathrm{numerator}(4B_{2k}/k)$ — the factor $4/k$, not $1/(4k)$. With the correct formula k=3 gives $496\cdot\mathrm{num}(2/63)=496\cdot 2=992$ ✓. Fix the exponent / factor placement.
- **`surgery-theory.html:336` — Whitney-trick "codimension ≥ 4" framing.** "In ambient dimension ≥ 5 a generic such [Whitney 2-]disk has self-intersections of codimension ≥ 4". The conclusion (Whitney trick succeeds when n≥5) is correct, but the codimension count is not standard: a generic 2-disk in ambient dim N has self-intersections of expected dimension $4-N$ (so empty for N≥5). Codimension in the disk is $N-2$ (=3 for N=5); codimension in the ambient is $2N-4$ (=6 for N=5). Neither is "≥ 4". Suggest rewording to "self-intersections have negative expected dimension, so generic position eliminates them."

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`surgery-theory.html:377` — "generated by the cyclotomic unit $1-t+t^2$" for $\mathrm{Wh}(\mathbb{Z}/5)\cong\mathbb{Z}$.** The element $1-t+t^2$ is a unit of $\mathbb{Z}[\mathbb{Z}/5]$ with augmentation 1 and is a known cyclotomic unit, but I can't crisply verify it generates Wh on its own (vs. a power or a related Bass cyclotomic unit). Conventional and harmless if cited from Bass, but a soft-cite.
- **`surgery-theory.html:457` — "$\mathbb{Z}$ (signature$/8$)" for $L_0(\mathbb{Z})$.** Convention-dependent: in Wall's formulation $L_0(\mathbb{Z})\cong\mathbb{Z}$ via signature/8 because the relevant forms are even unimodular, hence signature is divisible by 8. Some sources index by signature itself with the implicit understanding. Correct under the standard convention; a parenthetical "(forms are even, so signature ∈ 8ℤ)" would forestall confusion.
- **`surgery-theory.html:464` — "extra factor of $\mathbb{Z}/2$ in dimension 2, capturing the Browder–Livesay obstruction" for $L_*(\mathbb{Z}[\mathbb{Z}/2])$.** Wall's 1970 computation does have additional $\mathbb{Z}/2$ contributions beyond $L_*(\mathbb{Z})$, but the specific attribution to the Browder–Livesay invariant in dimension 2 is a soft cite — Browder–Livesay is more standardly tied to involutions / equivariant surgery. Plausibly correct via the relevant identification but not what most readers will recognize.
- **`surgery-theory.html:470` — "$E_8$ lattice has signature 8 but is not realised smoothly".** True under the convention that the form is taken positive-definite; the text earlier (line 520) calls $E_8$ negative-definite. Both signs appear in the literature; internally consistent if the reader notices the implicit sign flip, otherwise mildly confusing.
