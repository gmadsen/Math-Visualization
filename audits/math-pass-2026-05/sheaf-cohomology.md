## Wrong / dubious claims

- **`sheaf-cohomology.html:967`** — LES-stepper widget for the complex 1-torus (elliptic curve) labels `H¹(𝒪×) = Pic(X)` as the extension `0 → ℂ×/ℤ² → Pic(X) → ℤ → 0`. The connected component is $\mathrm{Pic}^0(E)\cong E\cong\mathbb C/\Lambda$ (additive quotient), which is $\mathbb C/\mathbb Z^2$, *not* $\mathbb C^\times/\mathbb Z^2$. As written, $\mathbb C^\times/\mathbb Z^2$ is non-compact and ill-typed (the $\mathbb Z^2$ action isn't even specified on $\mathbb C^\times$). Replace with `ℂ/ℤ² → Pic(E) → ℤ → 0`.
- **`sheaf-cohomology.html:825`** — six-functor sentence "for open immersions, $f_! = f_!$-extension by zero" has a typo: the right side should be "extension by zero" (without the second $f_!$). Trivial fix.
- **`sheaf-cohomology.html:582`** — "$H^0(\mathcal T_{\mathbb P^n})=\mathfrak{pgl}_{n+1}$." Standard notation is $\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}$ (the Lie algebra of $\mathrm{PGL}_{n+1}$ equals $\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}$ in char 0 / when $n+1$ is invertible). $\mathfrak{pgl}$ is non-standard — usually means $\mathfrak{gl}_{n+1}/\text{center}$, which is the same vector space but the notation $\mathfrak{sl}$ is universal in this context. Wording, not math.
- **`sheaf-cohomology.html:686`** — Pic of products: "Pic(X×Y) = Pic(X) ⊕ Pic(Y) ⊕ Corr(X,Y) (by the Künneth-style theorem)." There is no standard "Künneth theorem for Pic"; the correct statement (smooth projective over an algebraically closed field) is the seesaw decomposition $\mathrm{Pic}(X\times Y)\cong\mathrm{Pic}(X)\oplus\mathrm{Pic}(Y)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(\mathrm{Alb}(X),\mathrm{Pic}^0(Y))$. The page's "$\mathrm{Corr}(X,Y)$" is undefined and the attribution is inaccurate. Dubious / underspecified.

## Underspecified or unverifiable claims

- **`:584`** "Five-term exact sequence." The first five terms of the LES of a SES are correctly written, but the name "five-term exact sequence" canonically refers to the low-degree exact sequence of a Grothendieck spectral sequence (Hochschild–Serre etc.), not the LES of a SES. Naming clash, content correct.
- **`:739`** Serre duality stated for coherent $\mathcal F$ via $\mathcal F^\vee\otimes\omega_X$. The clean form requires $\mathcal F$ locally free; for general coherent $\mathcal F$ one needs $\mathcal Ext^i$ or the dualizing complex (acknowledged in §9 derived-Serre paragraph). Not wrong as written for the locally free case the page emphasizes; the paragraph's caveat covers the rest.
- **Mayer–Vietoris** — listed in audit focus but the page does not state a MV sequence. Coverage gap, not an error.
- **Hodge decomposition / Hodge numbers of K3, abelian surfaces** — listed in audit focus but not present on this page (handled in `algebraic-de-rham-cohomology.html`, linked at line 789). Coverage gap.
- **HRR in detail** — appears as a one-line statement (line 775) without proof, examples, or specific surface/3-fold computation. Statement itself is correct.
