# toric-varieties — pedagogical audit (2026-05)

**Section:** Algebraic geometry
**Compared against:** schemes, intersection-theory-chow

## Summary
The page is well-paced, voice-aligned with `schemes.html`, and richly interactive in §§1–5; the only substantive issues are duplicated callback asides in three sections (cosmetic, mechanical) and a worked-example gap in §6 "Mirror symmetry preview," which is otherwise pure prose-plus-formulas with no widget.

## Findings
### Notation drift
- `\mathrm{Cl}(X_\Sigma)` and `\mathrm{Cox}(X_\Sigma)` are written with `\mathrm{}` at lines 924, 925, 929, 930, 933, 944. The shared head macros (`\Spec`, `\Hom`, etc.) consistently use `\operatorname{}` across the trio. Cosmetic, but `\operatorname` is the house convention since it gets the spacing right after the operator name; either standardise on `\operatorname{Cl}` / `\operatorname{Cox}` or add `\Cl` / `\Cox` macros to the page head alongside `\Spec`.
- Inside widget option strings the page falls back to Unicode (`σ = ⟨e₁⟩` on line 529, `ℙ¹` on line 791) where peer pages prefer rendered KaTeX in `<select>` options via `js/katex-select.js`. `intersection-theory-chow.html` line 346 uses `$\mathbb{P}^1$` inside `<option>` and the page loads `katex-select.js`. Toric loads `katex-select.js` (line 178) but does not actually feed it any `$…$` options — Unicode is fine here, but worth flagging that the loader is dead weight if no LaTeX-bearing options exist. Low severity.
- `\mathrm{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2` (line 806) vs widget label `Bl₀ 𝔸²` and `Bl0` data-key (lines 791, 829, 851, 853): the same operator appears as `\mathrm{Bl}`, `Bl`, and `Bl₀` in adjacent paragraphs/widgets. Cosmetic.

### Undefined jargon
- "Gorenstein toric Fano surfaces" (line 1050) appears with no definition, no prereq callback, and no glossary aside. Reflexive polytopes are defined the paragraph above, but "Gorenstein" and "Fano" both arrive cold. Compare to `intersection-theory-chow.html` §6 where every new term (`Chern character`, `Todd class`, `K_0`) is defined before use in the same paragraph. Medium severity for §6.
- "anticanonical hypersurface" (line 1052, "A generic anticanonical hypersurface in $X_\Delta$ is a Calabi-Yau variety") is used twice without defining `K_X` or `-K_X`; readers from the `schemes` track will not have seen the canonical bundle. The callback at the bottom of §6 points to `calabi-yau-manifolds.html#cy-mirror-pair`, which is forward — no in-page anchor for a reader who hasn't yet read that page. Medium severity.
- "normal fan of a lattice polytope" appears in the §3 dictionary table (line 609) with zero in-page definition; first explanation is in §6 line 1052 ("rays are inner normals to facets, the maximal cones are normal cones at vertices"). Forward reference of a key term. Low–medium severity.
- "T-varieties," "log Calabi-Yau pairs," "tropicalizations" land cold in the §7 Connections paragraph (line 1076). Connections paragraphs in `intersection-theory-chow.html` and `schemes.html` either link out or stay in already-defined vocabulary. Low severity (connection prose is allowed to gesture).

### Tone mismatches
- Section 6 is the only section that drifts toward a textbook-recital voice ("Reflexivity is rare and rigid — there are exactly $16$ reflexive polygons in dimension $2$ … $4319$ in dimension $3$, and a famous Kreuzer-Skarke list of $473\,800\,776$ in dimension $4$.", line 1050). The numbers are great trivia but there is no widget or aside that lets the reader poke a single reflexive polytope. Compare `schemes.html` §2 hero ("we see factorization as the zero locus of a function") which earns its prose weight by being followed by the Spec ℤ widget.
- Otherwise voice is well-matched: "Now the parade." (line 786), "the bone-stock projective plane" (line 844), "A whole arithmetic family generated by tilting one ray" (line 806) have the same conversational-with-precise-payoff cadence as `schemes.html` §1 ("Three problems nag at you") and `intersection-theory-chow.html` §1 slogan-style asides.

### Missing worked examples
- **§6 "Mirror symmetry preview" has no widget.** Every other numbered section (§§1–5) has at least one interactive `<div class="widget">` (fan-builder, cone-table, orbit-cone, five-fans-five-varieties, intersection-table). §6 is six paragraphs of prose plus three displayed equations. Per AGENTS.md "every numbered `<h2>` section should have at least one concrete computation or widget." A small static SVG showing the 16 reflexive polygons in dimension 2 (or even just a clickable picker of the first 5) would patch this; a "type a reflexive triangle, see its dual" widget would be ideal. **High priority** within this audit.
- §6 also lacks a worked numeric example beyond the parenthetical "$(1, 101) \leftrightarrow (101, 1)$" for the quintic. `intersection-theory-chow.html` §6 (Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch preview) handles the same situation by walking GRR on $\mathbb{P}^2$ for $\mathcal{O}(d)$ in a 7-step proof-scrubber widget, recovering $\binom{d+2}{2}$. Toric §6 could do the same kind of step-through for the quintic Hodge swap.

### KaTeX macros / formatting
- No locally redefined macros beyond the shared head block — clean.
- One displayed-math hygiene nit: line 482 has `$$\sigma^\vee \;=\; \{\,u \in M_\mathbb{R} \;:\; \langle u, v\rangle \ge 0 \text{ for all } v \in \sigma\,\}.$$` — fine. Line 487 wraps `$$U_\sigma = \Spec \mathbb{C}[\sigma^\vee \cap M] = \Spec\mathbb{C}[S_\sigma].$$` inside `<p>…</p>` which is also fine, but in the surrounding paragraph at line 487 the `</p>` closes inside the same `<p>` that was opened on line 486 — the structure is `<p><strong>Definition.</strong> ... $$…$$</p>` (line 486–488). Compare `intersection-theory-chow.html` line 274 which puts the display math on its own `<p>` line. Cosmetic / parser-tolerant.
- **Duplicated callback asides** (high priority within this category, mechanical to fix): §1 has a hand-authored `<aside class="callback">` at lines 292–297 AND the auto-injected duplicate at lines 300–305 inside the `callback-auto-begin/end` fence. Same pattern in §3 (lines 626–631 vs 634–639) and §4 (lines 811–816 vs 819–824). Result: readers see the same "See also" block twice in those three sections. Per AGENTS.md "Callback idempotency", `audit-callbacks.mjs --fix` strips and re-inserts the auto block, but the hand-authored copies above the quiz are outside the fence and survive. Either delete the three hand-authored asides (preferred, keep the auto-managed copy) or add them to the prereq graph so they live inside the fence.
- Inline KaTeX inside table cells (§3 table at lines 599–612) renders fine but several cells use `\Sigma^{(1)}` notation that is introduced in the same row — fine, table is its own glossary.
