# mmp-and-birational-geometry — pedagogical audit (2026-05)

**Section:** Algebraic geometry
**Compared against:** resolution-of-singularities, positivity-and-ample-line-bundles

## Summary
Page is in strong shape: seven well-numbered sections, each with an interactive widget, narration that does the orientation work before formalism, and clean alignment with both peer pages on shared notation (`\overline{NE}(X)`, `N^1(X)_\mathbb{R}`, `K_X`, `\mathrm{Bl}_pX`). Polish issues are concentrated in two places — SVG-text Unicode that the peer pages also tolerate, and a small handful of jargon items (`$\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein`, `pseudo-effective`, `MMP with scaling`) that arrive without an inline gloss.

## Findings

### Notation drift
- Cone of curves: target writes `\overline{NE}(X)` everywhere (e.g. `#cone-theorem`: *"$\overline{NE}(X) \subset N_1(X)_\mathbb{R}$"*). `positivity#nef-big` matches: *"the closed convex cone generated by classes of irreducible curves"* with the same `\overline{NE}(X)` symbol. Aligned.
- Picard number: target uses both `\rho(X)` and `\mathrm{rk}\,N^1(X)` in the same sentence at `#surfaces` (*"the Picard number $\rho(X) = \mathrm{rk}\,N^1(X)$ by exactly $1$"*) — fine, since one defines the other; matches `positivity#nakai`'s `$\rho(X)$ is the Picard number`. Aligned.
- Blow-up symbol: target uses `\mathrm{Bl}_p\mathbb{P}^2` and `\mathrm{Bl}_{p,q} \mathbb{P}^2` in widgets/prose; `resolution-of-singularities#blowup` uses `\mathrm{Bl}_0 \mathbb{A}^n`, `\mathrm{Bl}_Z X`, `\mathrm{Bl}_p X`. Same `\mathrm{Bl}` convention throughout. Aligned.
- Canonical bundle: target uses `K_X` (e.g. `#kodaira-dimension`); both peers use `K_X` and `K_C`. Pluricanonical exponent style is consistent: `K_X^{\otimes m}` everywhere on target and `K_X \otimes L` on `positivity#vanishing`. Aligned.
- SVG-label Unicode (cosmetic): the cone widget at `#cone-theorem` uses `ℙ¹`, `𝔽_1`, `ℤ`, `(-1)-curve` in the readout/labels rather than KaTeX (e.g. `"K·E = -1"`). The same convention appears in `resolution-of-singularities#blowup` SVG and `positivity#ample` Serre table. This is the established convention for SVG-text — flagged here only for visibility, no action.
- `\dashrightarrow` (rational map) vs `\dasharrow`: target uses `\dashrightarrow` consistently (`#kodaira-dimension`: *"$\varphi_{|mK_X|} : X \dashrightarrow \mathbb{P}(...)$"*; `#sarkisov`: *"$f : X/S \dashrightarrow X'/S'$"*). Standard KaTeX. Aligned.
- "Picard rank one" phrasing: at `#sarkisov` the page writes `\rho(X/S) = 1` for relative Picard rank — clean. Slight prose inconsistency with `#flips` which writes "relative Picard rank $1$" in plain text instead of $\rho(X/Y) = 1$. Cosmetic.

### Undefined jargon
- "$\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier" appears at `#flips` (*"the canonical class $K_Y$ is not even a $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier divisor, so $\phi^* K_Y$ does not make sense"*) before being defined or callback-linked. The next section (`#singularities`) uses `\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier` again in the log-pair setup but still does not gloss it. Suggest one-clause inline ("$\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier — some integer multiple is Cartier") in either place.
- "$\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein" appears in the same `#flips` note (*"Then $Y$ is no longer $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein at the image points"*) without gloss. First time the term appears on the page. Either drop the term in favour of the `\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier statement that follows, or gloss it once.
- "pseudo-effective" first appears at `#surfaces` (*"$K_X$ is not pseudo-effective"*) with no gloss and no callback to `positivity-and-ample-line-bundles` (which defines the pseudo-effective cone in `#nef-big`). Worth a short callback or an inline gloss; the peer page uses `Pseudo-effective` as a labelled region in its widget, so the term is "owned" by `positivity` and a callback is the cheapest fix.
- "MMP with scaling" appears at `#flips` (*"…of one specific flavor (MMP with scaling) which is enough for finite generation"*) once, with no further explanation. Either drop the parenthetical or expand it with a sentence — currently it reads as jargon-name-drop.
- "abundance conjecture" appears at `#connections` (*"the abundance conjecture (that every nef minimal model has $|mK|$ basepoint-free for some $m$)"*) — gloss is provided in the same parenthesis. Good model for fixing the above items.
- "BCHM" appears in the `#kodaira-dimension` opening (*"finite generation of the canonical ring (BCHM 2010)"*) before the names Birkar–Cascini–Hacon–McKernan are spelled out — they are spelled out at `#flips` and again at the end of the same section. Consider expanding on first mention so the reader does not parse "BCHM" as an acronym whose meaning will be supplied 1500 words later.
- "$j$-invariant" in the Enriques table at `#surfaces` (*"$X \to C$ an elliptic fibration with non-trivial $j$-invariant"*) is unglossed; reader is expected to come from elliptic-curves background. Acceptable for this advanced-tier page.
- "Iitaka fibration" at `#kodaira-dimension` (*"varieties that fibre over a base of dimension $\kappa$ (the Iitaka fibration)"*) — adequate one-clause gloss.
- "klt" is given an inline expansion *(Kawamata log terminal)* in the table at `#singularities`, but the term is used twice earlier in the page — at `#flips` (*"Flips exist and terminate for klt threefolds"*) and again in the BCHM box (*"For klt pairs $(X, \Delta)$…"*). Consider either expanding on first use at `#flips` or rearranging so the table comes first.
- "$\phi$-ample" / `$\phi^+$-ample" at `#flips` (*"with $-K_X$ $\phi$-ample"*) is used without gloss, then explained obliquely in the readout. One-clause inline ("$\phi$-ample — ample on every fibre of $\phi$") would help.

### Tone mismatches
- Voice is mostly on-style: short paragraphs, conversational openers, concrete narration after each note. Hero `<p class="sub">` (*"Two birational varieties differ by a sequence of blow-ups, flips, and contractions… and the canonical bundle is its compass."*) sets the right Brilliant-style framing — comparable to `positivity-and-ample-line-bundles` (*"…almost every theorem in algebraic geometry lives at one of its faces"*) and `resolution-of-singularities` (*"Pinch a curve, crease a surface…"*).
- `#flips` "Existence and termination" subsection has a momentary dryness in the BCHM `<div class="note">`: the four-line definition is dense with named-conjecture references (klt, $K_X+\Delta$ big, $\Delta$ big, MMP with scaling) and would benefit from one bridging prose sentence after it ("Read this as: the local move (flip) and the global procedure (MMP with scaling) both work…"). The peers do this kind of post-note unpacking — `positivity#vanishing` follows its Kodaira note with *"The factor of $K_X$ is essential"*.
- `#sarkisov` "Birational rigidity" closer is excellent narrative voice (Iskovskikh–Manin, Lüroth problem). Aligned with `positivity#kodaira` Kodaira-embedding closer in tone.
- `#singularities` is the most formula-dense section and the prose-to-display ratio dips around the definition of `\mathrm{Diff}_S(\Delta')`. The "different" is named but not unpacked, and the iterated-adjunction sentence (*"…lets us run an MMP on the boundary itself, and is what makes induction on dimension viable"*) closes well but the formula immediately above lands as a wall. One worked toy adjunction (e.g. on a smooth surface with $S$ a curve) would soften it.
- `#connections` opens with a sharp single-paragraph framing (*"The MMP is the spine of higher-dimensional algebraic geometry…"*) before the bullet list — matches the pattern from `positivity#connections` exactly. Good.

### Missing worked examples
- Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 each ship an interactive widget. Coverage matches both peer pages.
- `#kodaira-dimension` widget plots $h^0(K_C^{\otimes m})$ as a function of $m$ on curves — solid concrete computation, comparable to the peer `positivity#picard` `O(d)`-on-`P^1` widget.
- `#cone-theorem` widget shows three concrete Mori cones (`Bl_pP^2`, `Bl_{p,q}P^2`, `P^1×P^1`) with click-to-read-contraction — strong worked example.
- `#surfaces` widget for iterated $(-1)$-contractions on $\mathrm{Bl}_{p_1,p_2,p_3}\mathbb{P}^2$ is concrete and clickable — good.
- `#flips` widget is schematic (a slider that opacity-fades $C \to \text{singular point} \to C^+$). It is illustrative rather than computational. Given that the textual content of `#flips` is the most demanding on the page, an additional very-small concrete example (e.g. the Atiyah flop $xy = zw$ in $\mathbb{A}^4$, with explicit $X^+$) would reinforce the abstraction. Low/medium priority.
- `#singularities` widget shows the resolution chain of $A_{n-1}$ singularities and reads off discrepancies = 0. Good concrete example, though every choice of $n$ produces the same `a = 0` — adding a non-canonical example (e.g. a `\frac{1}{n}(1, a)` singularity with a non-zero discrepancy) would let the reader see the discrepancy actually move. This would also concretely justify the four-class table that follows.
- `#sarkisov` widget shows the four link types schematically; the diagram for Type IV references "elliptic fibration on a Calabi–Yau threefold" but there is no concrete name dropped in the readout. Could borrow Atiyah-flop or Reye-congruence as a one-line example.
- `#connections` (section 7) is a navigation map, no widget — consistent with both peer `connections` sections.

### KaTeX macros / formatting
- Page-level `macros` block declares `\Spec, \Gal, \Hom, \tr, \ad, \ind` — verbatim copy of the canonical block in `category-theory.html` and matched in both peer pages. None of these are used in the target prose (the page lives in MMP-land, not Galois/Hom-land). No new macros introduced — clean.
- KaTeX delimiters used: only the standard `$…$`, `$$…$$` (auto-render adds `\(…\)`/`\[…\]`). No reinvention.
- `\overline{NE}`, `\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}`, `\mathrm{Exc}`, `\mathrm{Diff}_S`, `\mathrm{Bir}`, `\mathrm{Aut}`, `\mathrm{Pic}`, `\overline{\mathcal{M}}_g` — all standard KaTeX. No `\operatorname{Hom}`-vs-`\mathrm{Hom}` confusion (the page only uses operators that need it, and uses `\mathrm{}` consistently).
- Helper `<script>` block (`$`, `$$`, `SVG`, `ensureArrow`, `drawArrow`, `drawNode`) is the verbatim canonical block at lines 187–239. Spot-checked against `category-theory.html` lines 187–239 — byte-equal where it counts. No drift.
- Widget chrome: every interactive widget uses `<div class="widget">` + `<div class="hd"><div class="ttl">…</div><div class="hint">…</div></div>` + `<div class="readout">…</div>` + `<div class="row">` for controls. No ad-hoc classes. Note: the peer `resolution-of-singularities` widgets use `<span class="ttl">` instead of `<div class="ttl">` — both are valid per AGENTS.md "widget chrome" convention; target's `<div>` form matches `category-theory.html` and `positivity-and-ample-line-bundles`. Aligned.
- `<div class="note">` is the consistent wrapper for theorem/definition boxes throughout — matches both peers' usage. The page uses `.note` for both definitions and theorems (no `.def` / `.thm` split), consistent with house style.
- One small inconsistency: most `<div class="note">` blocks use `<p style="margin:.2rem 0">` to tighten the leading paragraph (matches `positivity` exactly), but the singularity-classes table at `#singularities` follows the table without a wrapping `.note` — fine, intentional.
- `<table class="plain">` for the contraction-types and Enriques tables — matches `resolution-of-singularities`'s ADE table and `positivity`'s usage. Aligned.
