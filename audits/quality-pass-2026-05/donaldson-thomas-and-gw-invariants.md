# donaldson-thomas-and-gw-invariants — pedagogical audit (2026-05)

**Section:** Algebraic geometry
**Compared against:** mirror-symmetry, calabi-yau-manifolds

## Summary
The page is dense, technically ambitious, and well-equipped with eight worked widgets; voice and KaTeX usage are largely consistent with the two reference peers. The most actionable defect is structural: every section carries a hand-authored `<aside class="callback">` *and* a near-identical auto-generated one, doubling the "See also" lists rendered on the page (high-priority, semantic).

## Findings
### Notation drift
- High priority. **Obstruction sheaf**: §2 first names it `$\mathcal{O}b$` (line 401: "the **obstruction sheaf** $\mathcal{O}b$"), then immediately switches to `$\mathrm{Ob}$` six lines later (line 407: "$\mathrm{Ob} = 0$") and the widget readout says `dim Ob = 0` again. Pick one — `\mathcal{O}b` is non-standard and KaTeX will typeset it as cal-O followed by an italic b. Recommend `\mathrm{Ob}` page-wide (matches widget readout text).
- Low priority. **Hilbert scheme of `$\mathbb{C}^2$` vs `C^2`**: §1 mixes `$\mathrm{Hilb}^n(C^2)$` (line 368, plain `C^2`) with `$\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\mathbb{C}^3)$` (same line, blackboard `\mathbb{C}`) in the same sentence. Calabi-yau-manifolds.html uses `\mathbb{C}` consistently. Settle on `\mathbb{C}^2`.
- Low priority. **DT generating series subscript style**: target uses `$Z_{\mathrm{DT}}(X;q,Q)$` (semicolon-separated) while mirror-symmetry uses `$Z_{\mathrm{GW}}(X;u,Q)$` (same convention) and `$F^{X}_0(q)$` (no semicolon). Internally consistent within DT page; cosmetic only.
- Low priority. **`$M$` overloaded.** §2 uses `$M$` for an abstract moduli space ("when a moduli space $M$ is singular …") while `$\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}(X,\beta)$` is used for the GW moduli space in §4. Mirror-symmetry uses the latter form too; the bare `$M$` is fine but readers may briefly conflate it with `\overline{\mathcal{M}}` two sections down.
- Low priority. **Curve-class hat**: §3 generating series writes `$Q^\beta$` while mirror-symmetry writes `$q^\beta$`/`$q^d$` (lowercase $q$ for the Kähler-class variable). Cross-page, the variable swap is justified (here `$q$` is the DT point-count variable), but a one-line cross-reference would help.

### Undefined jargon
- High priority. "**Hilbert scheme of `$n$` points**" (§1, line 277) is the first time the term is defined inside the page, but the *widget caption* immediately above (line 280) already says "Length-$n$ subschemes of $\mathbb{C}^2$ near the origin" — fine, but then the caption text in line 293 references `\mathrm{Hilb}^n` and `colength n` before the definition appears in the prose at line 277. Sequence is technically correct but tight; consider hoisting the one-line definition above the widget.
- Medium priority. "**categorified**" (§2 note, line 514) is dropped without gloss: "what makes DT theory 'categorified'". Neither the page nor the connections section defines what this means; a reader without a categorical background gets nothing from the term.
- Medium priority. "**BPS-state counts**" (§4, line 790) — first appearance, no explanation of what BPS means physically; the term is then used three more times (lines 794, 1142, 1168, 1171) before §8 says "BPS state counting in string theory" (still not defined). For a graduate notebook this is acceptable shorthand, but mirror-symmetry.html introduces "BPS counts $n_\beta$" with at least a parenthetical "(integer-valued)"; the target offers less framing.
- Medium priority. "**heart of a bounded `$t$`-structure**" (§7, line 1053) drops `$t$`-structure cold. No prereq callback, no inline gloss. Even granting that the reader has seen derived categories, a one-clause "(an abelian subcategory inside `$D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X)$` chosen to define what 'positive' means)" would help.
- Low priority. "**framing factor**" (§6 widget caption, line 946) and "**$\kappa(\eta)$**" appear before §6 defines `$\kappa(\lambda) = \sum_i \lambda_i (\lambda_i - 2i + 1)$` (line 931). Reorderable.
- Low priority. "**motivic Hall algebra**" (§7, line 1140) is named without prior context. One sentence later "**quantum dilogarithms**" appears, defined inline. Symmetry break.

### Tone mismatches
- Low priority. Section 1's lead sentence ("how many curves and points does $X$ contain, in each fixed homology class?") matches the conversational-but-precise voice of category-theory.html and mirror-symmetry.html. Most prose sustains this register.
- Medium priority. §6 final paragraph (line 1038) and §7 final paragraph (line 1142) drift into name-dropping wall-of-namespaces voice: "the operator-algebra approach to Hurwitz numbers — a deep web of unifications hiding under one Schur function" and "a quantum-cluster-algebra structure that connects DT theory to *cluster algebras*, BPS spectrum computations in `$\mathcal{N} = 2$` supersymmetric gauge theories, and stability conditions on the Fukaya category via homological mirror symmetry." Five concepts in one sentence with no anchoring computation. Calabi-yau-manifolds and mirror-symmetry generally end sections with a concrete claim or worked formula, not a name pile.
- Low priority. §7 widget readout text (line 1129–1132) reads as terminal-style ascii output ("Joyce / KS wall-crossing: ∏ U_α^{DT_-(α)} = ∏ U_α^{DT_+(α)}  in the motivic Hall algebra,"). That matches house style for `.readout` blocks (cf. mirror-symmetry's "F_0(q) = sum_d N_d q^d = …"); kept for consistency.

### Missing worked examples
- Every numbered `<h2>` has an interactive widget — coverage is good. The §3 MacMahon widget, §4 GW/DT-grid widget, §5 MNOP step-through, §6 toric-vertex picker, and §7 wall-crossing slider all give the reader a knob to turn.
- Medium priority. §8 (Connections) has no widget — but neither do the analogous Connections sections in mirror-symmetry or calabi-yau-manifolds, so this is on-pattern.
- Medium priority. §5 widget ("MNOP change of variables") is a 5-step click-through where the SVG content is text rendered as monospace (lines 880–884: `'font-family':'ui-monospace,monospace'`), not LaTeX. KaTeX cannot reach into dynamically-injected SVG `<text>` nodes, so the formulas read as ASCII (`(2 sin(du/2))^{−2}  =  − q^d / (1 − q^d)^2`). The widget's purpose — *seeing* the substitution — is partially undermined by the fallback rendering. Mirror-symmetry's §2 quintic table and §3 stable-map widget face the same KaTeX-in-SVG limitation but lean less on raw formulas in their SVGs. Cosmetic but worth noting.
- Low priority. The §7 widget shows wall-crossing schematically but does not actually compute either side of the Joyce/KS identity for a toy class — the "Watch DT counts jump" hint is aspirational; the readout does not show numerical jumps. Compare to mirror-symmetry's CdGP table which displays actual integers.

### KaTeX macros / formatting
- High priority. **Duplicate `<aside class="callback">` blocks.** Every section has a hand-authored callback block followed *immediately* by an auto-injected one (lines 370–376 + 378–386 in §1; 516–522 + 524–533 in §2; 660–665 + 667–674 in §3; 796–802 + 804–811 in §4; 1040–1045 + 1144–1149 + 1151–1158 in §6/§7). Total `<aside class="callback">` count: target = 11, mirror-symmetry = 6, calabi-yau-manifolds = 4. The hand-authored ones lack the `<!-- callback-auto-begin -->` fence so `audit-callbacks.mjs --fix` cannot strip them. Recommend deleting the manual blocks (they will be re-emitted by the injector from `concepts/donaldson-thomas-and-gw-invariants.json` prereqs). Source-of-truth fix is in `content/donaldson-thomas-and-gw-invariants.json`.
- Low priority. **KaTeX macros block** (lines 22–29) is a verbatim copy of the canonical block from category-theory.html and the references — `\Spec`, `\Gal`, `\Hom`, `\tr`, `\ad`, `\ind` — and the page does not introduce any local macros. Clean.
- Low priority. The page consistently uses `$…$` and `$$…$$` delimiters; no invented variants. `\boxed{…}` appears at lines 546 and 825 (DT-invariant and MNOP equality) — same usage pattern as mirror-symmetry's `\boxed{}` for the mirror-pair statement (line 272) and the HMS equivalence (line 459). On-style.
- Low priority. `\colon` vs `:` in maps: target uses `$f \colon C \to X$` (§4) and `$\phi \colon E^\bullet \longrightarrow \mathbb{L}_M$` (§2). Mirror-symmetry uses `$f \colon \Sigma_g \to X$` (§3). Consistent.
- Low priority. **Helper script block** (lines 187–239: `$`, `$$`, `SVG`, `ensureArrow`, `drawArrow`, `drawNode`) is a verbatim copy of the canonical 2D block from category-theory.html. No 3D helper block — none needed; all widgets are 2D SVG. Clean.
- Low priority. **Widget chrome.** Every widget uses `.widget / .hd / .ttl / .hint / .readout / .row / .pill / .note / .ok / .bad` from the house palette; no ad-hoc class names introduced. SVGs all carry `viewBox` and `<title>` elements (lines 291, 417, 572, 716, 840, 944, 1064). Clean.
- Low priority. SVG text fills mostly use `var(--*)` color tokens; one exception at line 325 (`stroke:'var(--orange,#ffb74d)'`) — `--orange` is *not* declared in this page's `:root` block (lines 37–42: only the original six accents). The fallback hex saves rendering, but `audits/coverage-stats.md` color-token rules would flag it. Either drop `--orange` (use `--yellow` or `--pink` to match the section palette) or add `--orange:#ffb74d` to `:root`.
