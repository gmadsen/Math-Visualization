# infinity-topoi — pedagogical audit (2026-05)

**Section:** Higher categories & toposes
**Compared against:** grothendieck-topologies-sites, infinity-categories

## Summary
Strong, well-paced capstone with consistent voice and notation across the three pages. The drift is concentrated in two places: (a) section 5 (Hypercompletion) is missing the "See also" callback block that every other section has, and (b) a handful of symbols (`\mathrm{Kan}_\infty`, `\mathcal{X}_\flat`, `\mathrm{Frob}_p`, "animae", "totalization") are used in prose / widget bodies before any local definition or peer-page anchor.

## Findings

### Notation drift
- **`\mathrm{Hom}` vs `\Hom` macro inconsistency inside the topoi page.** The topoi page declares `\Hom` in its KaTeX macros (line 25) and uses it in prose (`\Hom_{\mathcal{E}}(X, -)` in the table at §6, line 855), but section 6 also writes `\mathrm{Sub}(X) \cong \Hom(X, \Omega)` while §6's prose just below uses bare `\Hom`. The topoi scrubbers and the case-library widget consistently inline `\\Hom_{...}`. Low priority, but `grothendieck-topologies-sites` always uses `\Hom_C(-, c)` via the macro, never `\mathrm{Hom}` — settle on `\Hom` in topoi prose for consistency.
- **`\mathsf{Set}` vs `\mathbf{Set}` mixed.** Body prose at §1 line 278 uses `\mathsf{Set}` (matches grothendieck-topologies-sites and infinity-categories §1 line 322 which uses `\mathbf{Set}`). The "Used in" callback at line 291 and 286 in both pages serializes the cross-page link as `\mathbf{Set}`. This is consistent across the section but intentionally check: the `\mathsf{}` vs `\mathbf{}` split is an injector artifact, not a drift the author needs to fix.
- **`\widehat{C}` vs `\widehat C` (no braces).** §1 line 286 uses `\widehat{C}`, the §1-widget step-1 body (line 317) uses `\widehat C`. Cosmetic, but pick one — peers use `\widehat{C}` consistently.
- **`\mathcal{S} = \mathrm{Kan}_\infty`** (line 278). `\mathrm{Kan}_\infty` is a non-standard local abbreviation for "Kan complexes regarded as an ∞-category"; infinity-categories never introduces it. Either drop or define inline.
- **`\mathcal{X}_\flat`** appears in widget step 2 body of §5 (line 715, rendered as `𝒳_♭`) and is never defined on the page, in infinity-categories, or in grothendieck-topologies-sites. Likely meant the truncation/discretization — should be `\mathcal{X}_{\leq 0}` or `\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}` to match the §7 usage (line 952 uses `\tau_{\leq 0}`).

### Undefined jargon
- **"animae"** (§1 line 278): "Kan complexes / animae / homotopy types". Used as a synonym list before any of the three terms have been defined and "animae" is Clausen–Scholze terminology not used anywhere else in the corpus. Drop or footnote.
- **"saturated class"** (§2 line 379, "the saturated class generated by all such $R \hookrightarrow h_U$") — used without defining what saturation means in the localization context. infinity-categories §1 mentions "Joyal model structure" but never builds the saturation closure machinery a reader would need.
- **"totalization" / `\mathrm{Tot}`** (§2 line 381): "the equalizer with a totalization $\mathrm{Tot}(F(U_\bullet))$". First appearance of the term in the corpus, no callback to a definition; the construction "totalization of a cosimplicial diagram" is taken for granted.
- **"$\infty$-connective"** is defined inline in §5 prose (line 688) — good — but the §5 widget step 4 body uses it before the prose has been read in any non-linear order; minor.
- **"effective groupoid object" / "Segal-style horn-filling conditions"** (§3 G3, line 468): the prose says groupoid objects are "simplicial object[s] satisfying Segal-style horn-filling conditions" without explaining the Segal condition or pointing to simplicial-sets-and-nerve. Add a callback.
- **"$\ell$-adic sheaves" and `\mathrm{cd}_\ell`** (§5 prose lines 690, 694; case-library line 809) — used as if standard. The corpus has no étale-cohomology page wired in yet (the §7 callback to `etale-cohomology.html` is dangling — that file is not in the registry). Either drop the callback or call out the ad-hoc reference.
- **"Brunerie number"** (§6 sandbox preamble line 926, code line 937) — introduced parenthetically with no explanation; readers who haven't met it will treat the constant `2` as magic.
- **"Bhatt–Scholze prismatic formalism" / "pro-étale ∞-topoi"** (§7 line 956): name-drops at the close of the section, no callbacks. Acceptable for an "Open frontiers" coda, but currently sits inside a body paragraph.

### Tone mismatches
- **§1 opens at full speed.** Compare grothendieck-topologies-sites §1 ("If you have already met presheaves on a topological space …") which warms up via the second person and a familiar object; infinity-categories §1 ("A quasi-category is a simplicial set …") opens with a definition but immediately gives the "objects, morphisms, homotopies, homotopies between homotopies" slogan. infinity-topoi §1 jumps straight into `\mathcal{P}(C) := \mathrm{Fun}(C^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{S})` with the parenthetical "(Kan complexes / animae / homotopy types)" — terser and more textbook-voice than its peers. Consider one warm-up sentence.
- **§3 (Giraud axioms) is the densest section** — four numbered axioms in one `<ol>`, no inline example breaks. Peers break their axioms across paragraphs (grothendieck-topologies-sites §2 walks through M / S / T one paragraph at a time). Worked examples land only inside the widget; non-widget readers get a wall.
- **§6 internal-logic prose drifts toward survey voice** ("This is the most striking feature of higher topos theory"). Peers stay closer to the Riehl/Lurie clipped voice; the topoi page editorializes more in §6/§7/§8 than peers. Mild — fits the capstone framing.
- **§5 ends abruptly.** No transition between the case-library widget and the §5 quiz; peers always have a one-line wrap-up sentence after the final widget.

### Missing worked examples
- **§5 (Hypercompletion)** has two widgets but the prose doesn't compute a single concrete Postnikov tower or `\pi_n` sheaf. The case library tells you which ∞-topoi are hypercomplete but never walks an explicit ∞-connective non-equivalence. Adding a single computed `K(\mathbb{Z}/2, \infty)` Postnikov-tower-fails example would anchor the failure mode that the prose only describes.
- **§4 (Geometric morphisms)** opens with a concrete `j\colon U \hookrightarrow X` example in the prose but the widget is purely diagrammatic (clickable arrows, no instances). A second worked example — e.g. the étale geometric morphism `\mathcal{E}_{/X} \to \mathcal{E}` actually computed in `\mathcal{S}` — would parallel infinity-categories §5's concrete `\mathbb{L}\otimes^\mathbb{L} \dashv \mathbb{R}\Hom` mention.
- **§7 (étale ∞-topos of `\Spec\mathbb{Z}`)** has a great clickable widget but no closed-form computation of `H^*_{\mathrm{ét}}(\Spec\,\mathbb{F}_p; \mathbb{Z}/\ell)` or similar; the section asserts "natural home for $\ell$-adic sheaves" without ever computing one.

### KaTeX macros / formatting
- **`\mathrm{Kan}_\infty`** (§1 line 278) — bespoke; either macro it (e.g. `\Kan`) or write `\mathcal{S}` and drop the alias.
- **`\mathrm{Tot}`** (§2 line 381 prose; §2 widget step 6 body line 445) — used twice without a `\operatorname` wrapping; consistent with `\mathrm{Sh}`, `\mathrm{Map}`, `\mathrm{Lan}` style across all three pages, so OK by section convention. Flag only because it's unfamiliar — consider one inline gloss.
- **`\mathrm{Frob}_p`** (§7 widget line 1001) — same style as the rest, fine.
- **`\mathrm{ét}` (with combining accent inside `\mathrm{}`)** is used corpus-wide in this section; renders fine in KaTeX with `throwOnError:false`. Not a drift.
- **Subscript style for truncation: `\tau_{\leq n}` vs `\mathcal{U}_{\leq n}`.** Both used; consistent. Good.
- **§2 widget step 5 body (line 441) uses `<em>` inside a string literal** to mark "the extra axiom that distinguishes…" — every other widget body uses `<strong>` for emphasis bookmarks and `<em>` for variable names. Cosmetic.
- **`<!-- callback-auto-begin -->` block missing on §5.** Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 all carry the auto-injected callback block; §5 (lines 685–836) has none. Either §5's concept has no cross-topic prereqs (in which case fine) or `audit-callbacks.mjs --fix` needs another pass — verify against `concepts/infinity-topoi.json`.

## Severity
minor polish (one structural item — confirm §5 callback omission is correct against `concepts/infinity-topoi.json`; one symbol substitution — `\mathcal{X}_\flat` → `\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}`).
