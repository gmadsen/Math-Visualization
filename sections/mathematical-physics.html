Mathematical physics
The three-body problem
Newton's gravitational equations for three masses, Lagrange and Euler's special solutions, Lagrange points $L_1$–$L_5$, Smale horseshoe and KAM tori, modern spacecraft applications.
Newton · Lagrange points · KAM
Hamiltonians & classical mechanics
Symplectic phase space, Hamilton's equations, canonical transformations, Hamilton–Jacobi, Noether's theorem, Liouville integrability and KAM.
phase space · Noether · KAM
General relativity & Einstein equations
Pseudo-Riemannian 4-manifold, Einstein field equations $R_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2} g_{\mu\nu} R = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$, Schwarzschild and Kerr, FLRW cosmology, gravitational waves.
spacetime · Einstein · Schwarzschild · GW
Schrödinger equation
The state of a quantum particle is a unit vector in $L^2$, evolving by a one-parameter unitary group generated by the Hamiltonian. Stationary states, harmonic oscillator and hydrogen, scattering, and the path integral.
unitary evolution · stationary states · scattering · path integral
Statistical mechanics capstone
Microstates and ensembles, the canonical Boltzmann distribution, Gibbs entropy, the Ising phase transition and renormalization group, and large-deviations fluctuations — equilibrium thermodynamics from a probability measure on phase space.
Boltzmann · Ising · RG · large-deviations
Gauge theory capstone
Connections on principal bundles, curvature and the Yang-Mills action, gauge transformations and Wilson loops, instantons and Chern-Simons, lattice gauge theory, and the Donaldson / Seiberg-Witten 4-manifold invariants.
Yang-Mills · instanton · Chern-Simons · SW
String theory capstone
World-sheet action and 2D CFT, bosonic strings in $D=26$, superstrings in $D=10$, Calabi-Yau compactification, T-duality and mirror symmetry, branes — and the math output (mirror symmetry, moonshine, geometric Langlands).
CFT · D=10 · CY · T-duality · mirror
Special relativity
Two postulates, one new geometry: Lorentz transformations, the Minkowski metric, time dilation and $E=mc^2$, and the speed-of-light limit on causality.
Lorentz · Minkowski · γ · $E=mc^2$ · light cone
Klein-Gordon equation
The simplest manifestly Lorentz-invariant scalar wave equation. Quantizing $E^2=p^2+m^2$ delivers $(\Box+m^2)\phi=0$ — and with it antiparticles, a probability problem, and the bridge to the Schrödinger limit.
scalar QFT · Higgs · pions · □+m² · antiparticles
Dirac equation
The first-order relativistic spinor equation $(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0$. Fixes Klein-Gordon's probability problem, predicts antimatter, forces spin-½, and gives gyromagnetic ratio $g=2$ for free.
spinors · Clifford · γ-matrices · positrons · g=2
Quantum field theory
Capstone of the relativistic-physics arc: operator-valued fields on Fock space, the Feynman path integral, Feynman diagrams, renormalization-group flow, and gauged $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ — the framework behind every Standard-Model prediction.
capstone · path integral · Feynman diagrams · renormalization · Standard Model
