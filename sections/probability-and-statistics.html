DNA, RNA, and protein sequences as discrete objects: alignment via dynamic programming, search via the Burrows–Wheeler transform, evolution via HMMs and phylogenetic inference, and structure via energy-minimisation combinatorics.
alignment · BWT · HMM · phylogeny
